WHY ARE THERE SO MANY SYSTEMS?

Different traditions began from different questions.

One person can be read from more than one angle. Astrology begins with the sky and birth time; BaZi and Zi Wei grew from Chinese calendrical traditions; Numerology studies numerical patterns; Human Design uses a newer combined structural language; I Ching begins with change and situation; Tarot begins with images and the question in front of you. They remain distinct perspectives rather than seven votes on one identity.

THE PHI OS VIEW

Similarity is worth noticing, but it is not proof. Difference is not failure. A useful perspective gives you something clearer to compare with life.

REALITY COMPRESSION

Reality is too complex to read raw.

At any moment, reality can contain changes in time, emotion, identity, relationships, social structure, bodily state, collective movement and resource flow. A useful reading cannot carry all of that at full resolution. It has to select, organize and preserve a structure that can be examined.

TimeEmotionIdentityRelationshipsBodyEnvironmentResourcesCollectiveHistory

COMPRESSION IS NOT REDUCTION

It is a choice about what remains visible.

Calendars compress continuous time into cycles. Maps compress space into coordinates. Language compresses experience into concepts. Personal reading systems do something similar: they preserve selected relations so that a complex life can become inspectable without pretending the rest has disappeared.

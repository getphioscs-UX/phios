What PHI OS helps you see

A difficult situation is usually more than one isolated question.

A change in work can also affect money, confidence, relationships and timing. A quick answer may address one part while losing how the parts are connected.

PHI OS helps you describe the change, examine how it formed, understand the current picture, choose a bounded direction and later compare it with what actually changed.

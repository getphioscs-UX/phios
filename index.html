PHI OS Runtime

The intelligence is not one model. It is an architecture.

PHI OS keeps a changing reality connected from reconstruction and reading through bounded navigation, action, outcome and review.

Governed PHI OS Runtime Cycle

  1. 01 Input

    Begin with a question or a changing situation—not an automatic conclusion.

  2. 02 Reality Model

    Reconstruct events, relationships, constraints, evidence and unknowns as distinct components.

  3. 03 Reading

    Organize what is observed, experienced, derived and projected without collapsing their authority.

  4. 04 Navigation

    Form bounded options, constraints and signals. The system does not choose for you.

  5. 05 Action

    A human or authorized professional chooses what is actually carried into Reality.

  6. 06 Outcome

    Record what happened after action instead of treating advice as the result.

  7. 07 Review

    Compare expectation with outcome while conflicts, limits and unknowns remain visible.

  8. 08 Continuity

    Let the next reading begin from the changed Reality—not from a blank conversation.

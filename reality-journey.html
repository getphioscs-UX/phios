JR v2 · Canonical Journey Authority

Eight canonical workflow stages govern one Reality Journey.

The six customer tasks below remain a compatibility view. Canonical workflow state comes from JR v2, not from page order or page visits.

      One customer journey

      Six tasks, each with one clear purpose.

      Internal processing may use more detailed states, but they do not create a second journey for you to learn.

      Customer task projection

      Six customer tasks remain visible, but they are not a second Journey.

      They map through the frozen JR v2 compatibility registry and cannot redefine canonical stage order.

      1. 01

        Enter

        Your task

        Decide whether PHI OS can help with the change you are facing.

        What you leave with

        A clear choice to start, review your current status or leave without entering personal information.

      2. 02

        Describe

        Your task

        Say what changed, when it became noticeable and what it affects.

        What you leave with

        A description you can review and correct before moving on.

      3. 03

        Discover

        Your task

        Confirm how the situation developed, what influenced it and what is still unknown.

        What you leave with

        A corrected account of how the current situation formed.

      4. 04

        Understand

        Your task

        See what is happening now, why it matters and where confidence is limited.

        What you leave with

        A reality map that keeps evidence, interpretation and unknowns separate.

      5. 05

        Choose

        Your task

        Choose one bounded direction and decide what you will observe.

        What you leave with

        A next step and a review point, not a command or guaranteed result.

      6. 06

        Continue

        Your task

        Compare what you expected with what changed, then decide whether to continue, revise or close.

        What you leave with

        A reviewable history that changes only through your explicit choice.

      Journey boundaries remain active

      Emergency signals, regulated-advice requests, missing consent, professional escalation and data-purpose violations remain fail-closed checks. Journey recommendations are workflow steps only.

      Before you start

      Bring one change you can describe. You do not need a complete explanation.

      01

      What to enter

      Describe one recent change and avoid emergency information, passwords or identity numbers.

      02

      How saving works

      Entry may keep a protected recovery copy in this browser. Existing content stays hidden until you explicitly choose Resume.

      03

      Your current status

      Starting creates a new description. If you already started, check the dashboard before choosing whether to resume or begin again.

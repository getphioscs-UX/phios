# HD-PRO-R3-W19｜Epistemic + Sensitive-Domain Boundary

R3 customer prose may be conditional, descriptive, observational, reflective and explicitly open to counter-evidence. It may describe only the customer-supplied confirmed chart and admitted semantic/composition layers.

Sensitive domains remain reflection-only. Human Design does not diagnose or treat health or mental-health conditions, prescribe food or medication, guarantee financial/legal/safety outcomes, predict pregnancy or child destiny, score compatibility, or convert relationship structure into fate/dependency language.

A customer contradiction is valid reality evidence. The system must not force an interpretation to fit.

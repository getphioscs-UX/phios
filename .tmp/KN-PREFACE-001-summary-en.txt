为什么人工智能是文明能力长期累积的结果

Explains how artificial intelligence emerges from accumulated civilizational capabilities, including knowledge expression, material infrastructure, institutional coordination and feedback, while preserving the distinction between capability, direction, value and responsibility.
一个新的问题便自然出现：如果人工智能本身依赖整个文明系统才能形成，那么文明又是如何不断累积这些能力，并持续推动新的技术与新的现实共同出现的？

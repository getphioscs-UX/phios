### HERO｜同一件事，不只有一种理解方式。

**中文标题**

同一件事，不只有一种理解方式。

**English heading**

One situation. More than one way to look at it.

**中文引言**

生活中的一个问题，往往不只有一个层面。一份工作机会，可能同时关系到你的个人倾向、与家人的安排，以及眼前的现实条件。不同视角能帮助你发现不同的问题，理解原本没有注意到的部分。

PHI OS 让这些视角各自保留来源与边界。你可以用它们探索自己、理解关系或重新思考一个选择，但解释不会自动变成事实，也不会替你决定未来。

**English introduction**

A question in life rarely has only one side. A new job opportunity might involve your personal tendencies, family relationships, and the practical conditions you face today. Different perspectives can help you notice different questions and explore aspects you may not have considered.

PHI OS keeps each perspective connected to its source and limits. You can use them to explore yourself, understand relationships, or reflect on a choice. An interpretation does not automatically become a fact or decide your future.

### MODULE 01｜视角不是现实本身

**English heading**

A perspective is not reality itself.

**中文正文**

当你想理解一件事时，最先注意到的部分，不一定就是事情的全貌。有人首先在意实际成本，有人在意关系中的期待，也有人想通过一种象征方法重新理解自己的经历。

这些入口可以让思考变得丰富，却不能彼此替代。已发生的事实仍然是事实，个人感受有其自身意义，象征解释则属于它所使用的方法。理解得越深入，越需要知道自己正在使用哪一种来源。

**English body**

What you notice first is not necessarily the whole situation. One person may focus on practical costs, another on expectations within a relationship, while someone else may use a symbolic method to reflect on their experience.

These approaches can enrich understanding, but they are not interchangeable. Events and observations retain their factual status, personal experience has its own significance, and symbolic interpretations remain interpretations within their methods. Understanding improves when you know which kind of source you are using.

**结构图标题**

同一件事，不同层次的理解  
One situation, different layers of understanding

**图内标注**

|---|---|

| 观察与资料 | Observations and information |
| 所选择的视角 | Selected perspective |
| 形成的理解 | Interpretation |
| 返回现实核对 | Check against reality |

**图底说明**

解释可以帮助看见更多，但不能代替事实。  
An interpretation can reveal more without replacing the facts.

### MODULE 02｜先找到问题，再选择视角

**English heading**

Start with your question, then choose a perspective.

**中文正文**

如果你想进一步理解自己，可以从个人视角开始；如果你的疑问涉及伴侣、家庭或两个人之间的互动，可以进入关系视角；如果某个问题让你犹豫不决，也可以从易经或塔罗开始一段反思。

你不需要同时使用所有方法。重要的是先明白自己想了解什么，再决定哪一种阅读方式适合这个问题。

**English body**

If you want to understand yourself, begin with a personal perspective. If your question concerns a partner, family, or the dynamics between people, explore a relationship perspective. If you are reflecting on a particular uncertainty, I Ching or Tarot may offer another starting point.

You do not need to use every method. Begin with what you want to understand, then choose an approach suited to that question.

Explore I Ching or Tarot, subject to the experiences currently available.

### MODULE 03｜理解之后，重新看待选择

**English heading**

Let understanding inform your next choice.

**中文正文**

一种视角可能让你发现自己的顾虑来自哪里，也可能让你重新注意到某段关系或一个反复出现的问题。但理解不等于行动，行动也不代表结果已经确定。

你仍然可以回到现实，核对重要条件，比较不同方向的代价，再决定是否采取下一步。随着新的事情发生，原来的理解也可以被修正。

**English body**

A perspective may help you recognize the concerns behind a decision, notice something important in a relationship, or reconsider a recurring question. But understanding is not the same as taking action, and action does not guarantee an outcome.

You can return to the practical conditions, check what matters, compare possible trade-offs, and decide whether to take a next step. As circumstances change, your understanding can change too.

**结构图内标注**

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 发现新的问题 | Notice a new question |
| 理解一种视角 | Explore a perspective |
| 核对现实条件 | Check real-world conditions |
| 比较可能方向 | Compare possible directions |
| 自己作出选择 | Make your own choice |
| 观察变化，重新理解 | Observe change and reconsider |

**底部说明**

好的视角不会要求你相信一个确定答案，而是帮助你更清楚地提出问题。  
A useful perspective does not require belief in a certain answer. It helps you ask a clearer question.

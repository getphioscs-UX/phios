P02 · 一份关于财务，也关于生活的报告
A Financial Report Grounded in Everyday Life

现金流、资源、风险与行动，需要放回生活与责任的脉络中阅读。

Cash flow, resources, risk, and action become more meaningful when read in the context of everyday life and responsibility.

一个人的财务状况，并不会由账户余额、资产总额或某个月的结余独立决定。收入在不同时间进入生活，日常开销与家庭责任不断占用可用资金，借款、储蓄和投资则各有自己的期限与限制。有些资产在账面上具有价值，却未必能在需要时立即动用；有些支出发生在未来，却已经影响今天可以作出的安排。只有把这些情况放在同一个时间和责任背景中阅读，财务数字才开始具有可以理解的意义。

A financial position is never fully described by a bank balance, the value of one’s assets, or the surplus recorded in a single month. Income arrives at different times; living costs and household responsibilities draw on funds as they become due; borrowing, savings, and investments are shaped by their own terms and restrictions. Some assets have substantial recorded value without being readily available as cash. Some future commitments already influence what can reasonably be set aside today. Financial information becomes more useful when it is read together, with its timing, ownership, and obligations intact.

这份报告适合什么用途：自我梳理，更清晰地了解自己当前的财务状况与主要关注点。家庭讨论，为家庭成员之间关于财务与未来规划的讨论提供共同基础。专业准备，为后续与专业人士的沟通做好更充分的准备。

What this report helps you do: Clarify your current position and key areas of focus. Support family conversations about financial matters and future plans. Prepare for more effective discussions with professional advisers in the next steps.

这份报告不等于什么：不是自动投资建议。本报告不构成任何自动化的投资建议或交易指令。不是最终法律意见。本报告不构成最终的法律意见，也不替代您对相关法律事务的独立咨询。不是最终税务结论。本报告不构成最终的税务结论，也不替代您对相关税务事项的专业意见。

What this report is not: Not automatic investment advice or trading instructions. Not a final legal opinion and not a replacement for independent consultation on legal matters. Not a conclusive tax opinion and not a replacement for professional advice on tax matters.

本报告的四大核心板块：财务分析——理解现状，把握全局；资源规划——优化配置，释放潜力；风险评估——识别风险，稳健前行；行动路径——设定目标，迈向更好。

Four pillars of this report: Financial Analysis — Understand Your Finances. Resource Planning — Optimize Resources. Risk Assessment — Manage Risks. Strategic Next Steps — Turn Insights into Action.

P03 · 每一个数字，都有自己的来源与边界
Every Figure Has a Source — and a Limit

本报告基于客户提供的资料、结构化的输入信息及系统计算结果整理而成。每一个数字都来自特定的资料来源，并受到时间点、资产归属、资料完整性以及相关假设的影响。因此需要结合这些背景一起阅读，才能更准确地理解其含义。

This report is prepared based on information provided by the client, structured inputs, and system calculations. Every figure has a specific source and is subject to the timing, ownership, completeness of data, and related assumptions. Therefore, the figures should be read together with these background factors to fully understand their meaning.

数据处理流程：资料输入——客户提供的资料及结构化输入信息；系统整理与计算——系统进行分类、整理并基于设定进行计算；图表与说明——生成图表、关键数据及文字说明；待确认事项——标示需要进一步确认的资料或假设；行动与复核——与客户讨论，补充资料，并在需要时更新。

Data flow: Inputs — client-provided information and structured inputs. Structuring and calculation — data is categorized, organized and calculated based on system settings. Charts and interpretation — charts, key figures and written explanations are generated. Items requiring review — matters requiring further confirmation are highlighted. Action and review — discuss with the client, supplement data and update where necessary.

主要资料来源：收入与支出——工资、业务收入、其他收入以及日常与家庭支出等。资产与负债——现金、存款、投资、房产，以及各类负债（如房贷、贷款等）。储蓄与投资——定期存款、投资账户、退休储备及其他长期投资。保险与保障——人寿、医疗、重疾、意外等保障的既有配置与保费资料。家庭责任——家庭成员、教育、赡养及其他长期性的家庭责任。目标与专项规划——购房、子女教育、退休、创业等特定目标及专项规划安排。

Main data sources: Income and expenses — salaries, business income, other income and daily living and family expenses. Assets and liabilities — cash, deposits, investments, properties and various liabilities. Savings and investments — time deposits, investment accounts, retirement savings and other long-term investments. Insurance and protection — existing coverage and premium information. Household responsibilities — family members, education, elderly support and other long-term responsibilities. Goals and special planning — home purchase, children’s education, retirement and business planning.

资料状态说明：已确认——基于客户提供的明确资料，并经过核对后使用。估算——基于有限资料，按照合理方法进行估算。缺失——目前尚未提供相关资料，暂无法纳入分析。情景假设——基于特定假设条件，用于情景分析，不代表已发生或确定的结果。

Data status markers: Confirmed — based on clear information provided by the client and used after verification. Estimated — derived from limited information using reasonable assumptions and methods. Missing — relevant information has not been provided yet and is not included in the analysis. Scenario-based — based on specific assumptions for scenario analysis; does not represent actual or confirmed outcomes.

如何理解这些数字：报告日期与资料截止点很重要；货币与时间周期需要特别留意；零不等于未知，缺失表示目前没有相关资料；估算和情景不等于已确认结果。财务报告的价值，不在于假装每一个数字都确定无误，而在于清楚地展示哪些是已知的、哪些是基于合理推断的，以及哪些仍需进一步确认。

How to read these figures: Report date and data cutoff matter. Currency and period must be read carefully. Zero is not the same as unknown. Estimates and scenarios are not confirmed results. A financial report becomes more useful not by pretending that every figure is certain, but by clearly showing what is known, what is inferred, and what still needs confirmation.

P04 · PHI OS 财务视角
PHI OS Financial Lens

从现金流到选择：财务的四个观察方向

From Cash Flow to Choice: Four Ways of Reading a Financial Life

财务并不只是看一个数字够不够，而是看钱如何进入生活、如何流出，哪些资源能够支撑当下与未来，哪些限制正在形成，以及下一步应当先处理什么。PHI OS 的财务视角把这些关系放回同一张图中，让阅读不只停留在余额、总资产或单个月结余，而是看见生活、责任、时间与选择如何共同构成一个人的财务现实。

Finance is not understood through a single number. It becomes clearer when we see how money enters life, how it leaves, which resources support the present and the future, which constraints are emerging, and what should be addressed first. The PHI OS financial lens places these relationships back into one view, so that reading moves beyond a balance, a total asset figure, or a single monthly surplus, and begins to show how life, responsibility, time, and choice together form a financial reality.

现金流：看收入、支出、偿债与结余之间如何彼此影响。它不只是记录钱进钱出，更帮助理解生活安排如何持续、哪些地方正在消耗余裕、哪些变化会立刻影响可用空间。

Cash flow: Observe how income, expenses, debt service, and surplus affect one another. It is not only a record of money in and money out, but a way to understand how life is being sustained, where flexibility is being consumed, and which changes would immediately alter available room.

资源：看资产、储蓄、投资、保障与家庭支持系统如何形成可动用与不可动用的资源结构。重点不只在总额，而在于结构、流动性、期限与配置。

Resources: See how assets, savings, investments, protection, and household support create a structure of usable and less-usable resources. The focus is not only on total value, but on structure, liquidity, timing, and allocation.

风险：看负债、流动性不足、保障缺口与未来不确定性如何构成限制。风险不是为了制造焦虑，而是为了更早辨认压力来自哪里，以及哪些脆弱点需要先补强。

Risk: Identify how liabilities, limited liquidity, protection gaps, and future uncertainty create constraints. Risk is not used to create fear, but to recognize earlier where pressure is coming from and which vulnerabilities need to be strengthened first.

行动：看洞察如何转成排序、选择与执行。行动不是一次做完所有事情，而是依据当前现实与目标，先处理最关键的一步，再逐步推进后续安排。

Action: Turn insight into sequence, choice, and execution. Action does not mean doing everything at once; it means responding to current reality and future aims by taking the most important next step first, and then progressing in order.

例如，贷款提前还本可能降低长期利息，却会占用当前现金与储备；增加储蓄能强化缓冲，却可能延后短期消费与其他目标。财务判断之所以困难，往往不是因为数字本身，而是因为不同面向同时牵动彼此。

For example, early loan repayment may reduce long-term interest while drawing on present cash and reserves; increasing savings may strengthen resilience while delaying short-term spending or other goals. Financial judgment is often difficult not because numbers are unclear, but because different directions affect one another at the same time.

P05 · 如何阅读本报告
How to Read This Report

先看全貌，再看关系，最后回到自己的生活。

Start with the Whole, Follow the Connections, Return to Life.

这份报告并不是要用单一数字替你下结论，而是帮助你把收入、支出、资产、负债、目标与未来安排放回同一脉络中理解。阅读时，建议先看全貌，再看各部分之间的关系，最后回到你眼前真正需要处理的生活问题。

This report is not designed to let a single number speak for your whole life. It is meant to help you read income, spending, assets, liabilities, goals, and future plans in one connected context. Begin with the whole picture, then follow the relationships between the parts, and finally return to the real-life decisions that matter now.

阅读顺序：报告总览 → 财务分析 → 资源规划 → 风险评估 → 行动路径。先看整体，再逐步进入各板块，最后回到行动。

Reading path: Report Overview → Financial Analysis → Resource Planning → Risk Assessment → Strategic Next Steps. Start with the overall picture, move through each section, and end with action.

如何理解图表与数字：所有图表都基于特定资料版本，之后发生的变化未必包含在内。请留意金额所使用的币种，以及数字代表的是月度、年度还是特定期间。不同状态代表不同的确定程度，不能混为一谈。有些页面呈现现状，有些比较目标，也有些仅用于情景理解。

How to read charts and figures: All figures reflect a specific reporting version; later changes may not be included. Note the currency used and whether a figure refers to a monthly, annual, or specific period result. Different markers represent different levels of certainty and should not be treated as the same. Some pages describe the current position, some compare goals, and others illustrate scenarios.

资料状态与图例：已确认——资料已核实，可信度较高。估算——基于现有资料推算，仍有不确定性。缺失——目前暂无资料，可能影响完整性。情景假设——基于特定假设，用于情景分析与理解。估算与情景并不等于错误；它们只是提醒你这些结果仍需结合条件理解。

Status markers and legend: Confirmed — data has been verified and is considered reliable. Estimated — based on available data and subject to uncertainty. Missing — data is currently unavailable and may affect completeness. Scenario-based — based on specific assumptions and used for scenario analysis and illustration. Estimated and scenario-based figures are not errors; they indicate dependence on assumptions or limited information.

如何使用这份报告：先确认最重要的问题——现金流、目标、风险，还是资料缺口。再看相关关系——收入、支出、资产、负债或家庭责任与它有何关联。最后决定下一步——补资料、调整安排、重设优先目标，或准备进入专业讨论。图表的价值，不在于让你一次看完所有数字，而在于帮助你知道下一步该看什么、确认什么、处理什么。

How to use this report: Identify the main issue first — cash flow, goals, risk, or missing information. Follow the linked factors — income, spending, assets, liabilities, or household responsibilities. Turn insight into action — fill in missing data, adjust arrangements, reset priorities, or prepare for professional discussion. The value of a report is not that you read every number at once, but that you know what to look at next, what to confirm, and what to act on.

P21 · 资料、方法与适用边界
Methods, Evidence & Boundaries

每一项计算都需要有依据，每一段说明都应展示其适用范围。报告基于特定时间、特定资料版本及明确计算条件。收入、支出、资产与负债只有在币种、期间、所有权及资金用途区别后，才具有可比较意义。部分结果来自客户确认的资料，部分由系统按既定方法计算，部分属于估算或客户选定的情景；不同结果不具有完全相同的确定程度。

Every calculation needs evidence, and every explanation should show its scope. The report uses a defined date, information version and calculation conditions. Currency, timing, ownership and intended use must be distinguished before financial figures can be compared meaningfully. Client information, established calculations, estimates and selected scenarios do not all carry the same degree of certainty.

报告日期见封面；资料使用对应保存版本；币种按各项结果说明；情景须明确声明。当前旧底图包含示例身份及待办，前景已中性化；这不替代批准空槽位资产。

See the cover for the reporting date; use the corresponding saved version; read currency with each result; scenarios require explicit declaration. The current old artwork contains example identity fields and tasks, neutralized in the candidate foreground. This is not approval of an empty-slot asset.

现金流计算：按既定期间与币种计算收入及支出，已包含偿债的还本付息不得再次扣除。净资产计算：资产与负债按性质分类，共同持有资产按相应权益份额计入。储备覆盖：取决于可动用资金的范围及所选期间，不同期限与情景可能导致不同结果。

Cash flow uses the defined period and currency; debt servicing already included in expenses is not deducted again. Net assets classify assets and liabilities by nature and use the respective ownership shares. Reserve coverage depends on accessible funds and the selected period; horizons and scenarios may lead to different results.

资料来源与状态：客户提供资料，系统按既定方法处理，再进行结果说明与复核。客户确认、系统计算、估算、情景假设和待核实是不同状态，不表示所有资料均已获独立专业确认。

Source and status: client input is processed using established methods, interpreted and reviewed. Client-confirmed information, system calculations, estimates, scenario assumptions and pending verification are distinct; they do not establish independent professional verification of every fact.

使用与专业边界：报告用于整理资料、计算及情景说明，不构成自动投资建议、最终法律意见或最终税务结论。部分事项仍需专业复核。本次待确认区不预先放入固定客户判断，正确空槽位素材仍待提供。

Use and professional boundaries: the report organizes information, calculations and scenarios. It is not automatic investment advice, a final legal opinion or a final tax conclusion. Some matters require professional review. No fixed client judgments are prefilled into the follow-up slot; the correct empty-slot artwork is still pending.

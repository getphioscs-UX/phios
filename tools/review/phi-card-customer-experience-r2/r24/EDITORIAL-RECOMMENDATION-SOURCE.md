## 三、P01–P08 — 16 条精简、独立的顾客阅读边界候选

以下仅替换卡片的顾客可见 `sourceBoundary`；被冻结的 Registry、技术 lineage 和审计原文保持原样。

### P01 · 开启 | OPENING

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡提供关于新可能的观察角度，并不表示某个机会已经在现实中出现，也不保证它会实现。是否值得尝试，仍需核对实际条件与可用资源。

**en：** This card offers a perspective on an emerging possibility. It does not confirm that an opportunity has appeared in your life or guarantee an outcome. Any next step should be guided by actual conditions and available resources.

### P02 · 启动 | ACTIVATING

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡帮助观察从准备进入初步行动的阶段，不表示你的计划已经真实启动，也不能证明它已经稳定。请以实际完成的步骤与可重复验证的结果判断。

**en：** This card invites reflection on moving from preparation into initial action. It does not confirm that your plan has started or become reliable. Look for actions that have actually occurred and results that can be verified again.

### P03 · 成势 | GAINING WEIGHT

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡关注某种模式影响力增加的可能性，不表示你的事业、关系或计划正在取得成功，更不保证增长会持续。实际变化仍需通过成果、反馈与资源条件确认。

**en：** This card explores the possibility of a pattern becoming more influential. It does not establish success in your work, relationships, or plans, or promise sustained growth. Evaluate changes through actual outcomes, feedback, and available resources.

### P04 · 放大 | AMPLIFYING

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡关注环境变化如何让原有模式更明显，并不表示现实中的优势或问题必然正在增强。更容易被看见，不等于更准确；仍需检查实际原因与证据。

**en：** This card considers how changing conditions can make an existing pattern more visible. It does not establish that a real-world strength or problem is increasing. Greater visibility does not mean greater accuracy; examine the evidence and its causes.

### P05 · 对齐 | ALIGNMENT

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡提供对条件暂时配合的观察角度，不表示你的现实正处于有利时机，也不保证顺畅会持续。重要决定仍需核对资源、责任及实际结果。

**en：** This card offers a way to consider conditions working together with less friction. It does not confirm that circumstances in your life are favorable or that they will remain so. Important decisions still require evidence about resources, responsibilities, and outcomes.

### P06 · 过度放大 | OVERAMPLIFICATION

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡提醒留意投入强度与实际效果是否失衡，不表示你已经过载，更不是负面预兆。是否需要降低强度，应依据实际成本、容量及反馈判断。

**en：** This card invites you to examine whether greater intensity is still producing useful results. It does not establish that you are overloaded or signal a bad outcome. Decisions about reducing intensity should reflect actual costs, capacity, and feedback.

### P07 · 回落 | DECAY

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡提供对活动强度回落的观察角度，不表示你的事业、关系或健康正在衰退，也不预测某件事即将结束。这里的「能量」指可用资源与支持，并非医学测量；实际调整应依据事实与责任。

**en：** This card offers a perspective on activity becoming less intense. It does not establish decline in your business, relationships, or health, or predict an ending. Here, “energy” refers to available resources and support, not a medical measurement; decisions still require evidence and regard for obligations.

### P08 · 收束 | CLOSURE

**zh-Hans：** 这张卡提供整理当前阶段的观察角度，并不表示你的事业、关系或计划即将结束，也不预测何时终结。若需真正关闭或交接，应确认已完成成果、持续义务及相关人员的权益。

**en：** This card invites reflection on bringing a stage to an appropriate conclusion. It does not predict that your business, relationship, or plan will end. Actual closure or handover requires confirmation of completed work, continuing obligations, and the rights of those involved.

# F01 — 容器 | CONTAINER

**Field · 场域条件**

**卡片副标题 | Card Subtitle**

复杂内容需要一个足够稳定的承载空间。

Complex material needs a stable enough container.

---

## 一句话理解 | At a Glance

**有些事情不需要更多努力，而是需要一个稳定的环境，让重要的资料、责任或计划能够被妥善安排，不必一直依靠临时处理。**

Sometimes what matters is not greater effort, but a stable environment where information, responsibilities, and plans can be managed without constant intervention.

## 这张卡想提醒什么？

_What This Card Invites You to Consider_

当生活或工作变得复杂，缺少稳定的安排可能让每件事都需要重新协调。

「容器」提醒你，一个适当的支持环境，可以是清楚的工作流程、可靠的资料管理、固定的家庭安排，或能够专心处理重要事务的空间。

好的容器不需要解决所有问题，而是让不同事情有适当的位置，并允许它们逐步完成。

As life or work becomes more complex, a lack of stable arrangements can make everything require repeated coordination.

Container invites you to consider whether a clearer workflow, reliable information system, consistent household routine, or suitable workspace could provide needed stability.

A good container does not solve every problem immediately. It creates appropriate conditions for important things to be managed and developed.

## 现实中可能如何出现？

_How It May Appear in Real Life_

**工作 | Work**

团队拥有许多重要资料，却没有统一的存放和查找方式。建立清楚的记录与管理权限，可能比不断增加沟通更有效。

A team has valuable information but no consistent way to store or retrieve it. Clear records and appropriate access controls may help more than additional communication.

**家庭 | Family**

家庭成员经常临时协调孩子的时间。建立适当的日常安排与备用方案，可以减少重复讨论。

Family members repeatedly coordinate children's schedules at the last minute. A workable routine and backup arrangements can reduce unnecessary coordination.

**个人 | Personal**

如果一项学习计划总被其他事情中断，固定的学习时间和适当的环境，可能比购买更多教材更有帮助。

If learning is repeatedly interrupted, protected study time and a suitable environment may be more useful than purchasing additional resources.

## 什么做法更有帮助？

_A Helpful Approach_

**先建立稳定的支持条件，再增加新的要求。**

确认重要事项需要什么空间、资料、时间或管理方式，让它们能够持续进行。

**Establish reliable supporting conditions before adding new demands.**

Identify the space, information, time, or arrangements needed for important activities to continue.

## 什么情况需要留意？

_What to Watch For_

容器如果缺少边界，也可能变成无限承接。不是所有新任务都必须加入同一套安排。

**稳定的容器应该保护容量，而不是不断扩大负担。**

A container without boundaries can become an arrangement expected to hold everything.

**A stable container should protect capacity rather than continually absorb new demands.**

## 现在可以做什么？

_One Small Action_

1. 选择一件经常需要重新协调的事情。
    
2. 找出它缺少什么稳定条件。
    
3. 建立一个简单的流程、固定空间或记录方式。
    
4. 观察是否减少了重复处理。
    
5. Choose something requiring repeated coordination.
    
6. Identify the missing supporting condition.
    
7. Establish a simple process, dedicated space, or record.
    
8. Observe whether repeated effort decreases.
    

## 留给自己的问题

_Your Reflection Question_

**什么样的空间会让你更容易把事情放稳，而不是一直应付？**

**What kind of space helps you hold things steadily instead of constantly reacting?**

---

_这张卡描述的是可能有助于稳定运行的环境条件，不代表系统已确认你的实际生活或工作环境存在问题。_

_This card presents possible environmental conditions that support stability. It does not establish that your actual living or working environment has a problem._

# F02 — 边界 | BOUNDARY

**Field · 场域条件**

**卡片副标题 | Card Subtitle**

清楚的界线让运行知道在哪里停止与稳定。

Clear limits show the runtime where to stop and stabilize.

---

## 一句话理解 | At a Glance

**真正有效的边界，不是把所有事情挡在外面，而是让时间、资源与责任拥有清楚范围，使重要的事情能够稳定进行。**

Effective boundaries do not shut everything out. They establish clear limits for time, resources, and responsibilities so that important activities can function reliably.

## 这张卡想提醒什么？

_What This Card Invites You to Consider_

生活中，许多困难并不是因为我们不愿意合作，而是因为责任与要求的范围不够清楚。

工作中，新任务不断加入，却没有明确优先次序；家庭里，某个人持续承担原本没有约定的责任；个人时间也可能被各种临时要求占据。

「边界」提醒你：适当的支持，有时候并不是接受更多，而是让各方清楚知道什么可以安排、什么需要协商，以及什么已经超出合理范围。

好的边界能减少误解，同时保留必要的合作与变化空间。

Many difficulties arise not from an unwillingness to cooperate, but from unclear limits around responsibilities and expectations.

At work, additional tasks may accumulate without agreed priorities. At home, someone may gradually take on responsibilities that were never clearly discussed. Personal time may also become occupied by constant requests.

Boundary invites you to recognize that support sometimes comes from clearer limits rather than greater availability.

Effective boundaries reduce misunderstandings while preserving room for appropriate cooperation and change.

## 现实中可能如何出现？

_How It May Appear in Real Life_

**工作 | Work**

一个团队不断收到新的功能要求，导致原定项目反复中断。

明确当前开发范围、必要修复与未来功能的处理方式，可以保护核心交付，同时保留后续改善的机会。

A team receives constant feature requests, repeatedly interrupting planned work.

Defining current scope, essential fixes, and future development procedures can protect delivery while allowing later improvements.

**家庭 | Family**

家庭成员可能习惯由同一个人协调所有事务，却没有明确各自的责任。

通过适当沟通确认分工、时间和备用安排，可以让共同生活更加稳定，而不是把所有协调留给一个人。

Family members may rely on one person to coordinate nearly everything without clearly defined responsibilities.

Agreeing on roles, schedules, and backup arrangements can make everyday life more reliable without concentrating all coordination on one individual.

**个人与财务 | Personal Life & Finances**

一个人可能经常收到借款、协助或其他资源要求，却没有明确自己能够合理提供多少。

清楚说明可用资源和个人责任范围，可以保护必要的财务安排，也避免作出无法持续履行的承诺。

Someone may receive frequent requests for loans, assistance, or other resources without clearly defining what they can reasonably provide.

Communicating available capacity and personal limits can help protect essential financial arrangements and prevent commitments that cannot be sustained.

## 什么做法更有帮助？

_A Helpful Approach_

**让边界具体、清楚，并与现实责任保持一致。**

与其笼统地说「不要再增加事情」，不如明确说明当前能够接受什么、需要怎样的协商，以及哪些事项必须按照已有责任继续处理。

边界也可以随实际条件改变而重新检查，不需要永远维持同一种安排。

**Make boundaries specific, understandable, and consistent with real responsibilities.**

Rather than simply saying "no more requests," clarify what can be accepted, what requires discussion, and which obligations must still be fulfilled.

Boundaries can be reviewed as circumstances change. They do not need to remain identical forever.

## 什么情况需要留意？

_What to Watch For_

边界最大的风险，是从必要保护逐渐变成拒绝所有变化。

如果任何新建议、资讯或支持都被排除，原本帮助稳定的规则，也可能阻碍进一步改善。

另一方面，边界不应只停留在口头表达。如果缺乏实际分工、时间安排或执行机制，同样的问题可能持续出现。

**有效的边界既能保护重要资源，也允许经过适当判断的新需要进入。**

A risk of boundaries is allowing necessary protection to become resistance to all change.

When every new suggestion, piece of information, or form of support is excluded, rules that once provided stability may begin preventing improvement.

Boundaries also need practical implementation. Without clear responsibilities, schedules, or procedures, the same difficulties may continue.

**Effective boundaries protect important resources while allowing appropriate new needs to be considered.**

## 现在可以做什么？

_One Small Action_

1. 选择一件责任或要求不够清楚的事情。
    
2. 确认自己实际需要负责的范围。
    
3. 向相关人员说明可以接受什么、哪些需要另行协商。
    
4. 观察沟通与执行是否变得更清楚。
    
5. Choose a situation with unclear expectations or responsibilities.
    
6. Identify the scope you are genuinely responsible for.
    
7. Communicate what is manageable and what requires further agreement.
    
8. Observe whether coordination and execution become clearer.
    

## 留给自己的问题

_Your Reflection Question_

**什么界线一旦说清楚，会让整个环境更容易运作？**

**Which boundary, if made clear, would make the whole environment easier to operate in?**

---

_这张卡描述的是可能有助于稳定运行的边界与环境条件，不代表系统已经确认你的个人关系或工作安排存在边界问题。合理边界仍须尊重实际承诺、法律责任、安全与相关人员的权益。_

_This card presents boundary conditions that may support stability. It does not establish that your relationships or work arrangements have boundary problems. Appropriate limits must remain consistent with actual commitments, legal obligations, safety, and the rights of those involved._

# F03 — 协作 | COLLABORATION

**Field · 场域条件**

**卡片副标题 | Card Subtitle**

不同位置通过分工与互补形成更好的承载。

Different positions create better capacity through complementarity.

---

## 一句话理解 | At a Glance

**好的协作，不是增加更多参与者，而是让不同的人发挥适当的作用，通过明确分工与互相配合，使重要事务能够更稳定地完成。**

Effective collaboration is not simply about involving more people. It brings together complementary capabilities and clear responsibilities so that important work can be completed more reliably.

## 这张卡想提醒什么？

_What This Card Invites You to Consider_

生活中，有些困难并不是因为人手不足，而是现有的能力与责任没有被合理安排。

一个团队可能拥有足够的专业人员，却因为分工不清而重复工作；家庭成员可能都愿意帮忙，却不知道谁负责什么；一项计划也可能长期依靠同一个人完成所有协调。

「协作」提醒你：**真正的支持，有时候来自更清楚的角色与互补，而不是要求每个人付出更多。**

好的协作允许不同的人拥有不同的能力和责任，同时建立清楚的交接与沟通方式。

真正需要改善的，不一定是参与人数，而是如何让已有资源在适当的位置发挥作用。

Some difficulties arise not from too few people, but from unclear responsibilities and poorly arranged capabilities.

A team may have sufficient expertise yet duplicate work because roles overlap. Family members may be willing to help without knowing who is responsible for what. A project may depend excessively on one person for coordination.

Collaboration reminds you that **meaningful support often comes from clearer roles and complementary contributions rather than asking everyone to do more.**

Effective collaboration allows people to contribute differently while maintaining clear communication and handovers.

The solution may lie less in increasing participation and more in arranging existing resources appropriately.

## 现实中可能如何出现？

_How It May Appear in Real Life_

**工作 | Work**

生产、财务和管理部门可能各自拥有完整资料，却因为责任与交接方式不清楚，需要不断重复核对。

明确哪个部门负责输入、谁负责验证，以及结果交给谁使用，可以改善协作，而不必让所有人同时处理相同工作。

Production, finance, and management may each possess useful information, yet unclear responsibilities create repeated reconciliation.

Defining who records information, who verifies it, and who uses the results can improve collaboration without making everyone perform identical tasks.

**家庭 | Family**

家庭可能长期由一人负责孩子安排、家务和重要文件，其他成员则偶尔协助。

更有效的协作，可以是根据实际时间与能力明确分工，让不同成员承担适合的责任，并建立必要的备用安排。

A household may rely on one person for childcare schedules, domestic tasks, and important documents while others help occasionally.

Better collaboration may involve assigning responsibilities according to available time and capabilities, with appropriate backup arrangements.

**个人与专业合作 | Personal & Professional Cooperation**

一个人可能擅长设计和整理内容，却缺少某些技术或专业能力。

与适当的合作伙伴分工，可能比尝试独自掌握所有技能更有效。涉及专业资格或客户资料的工作，还必须确认合法权限与保密要求。

Someone may be capable in design and content organization but lack particular technical or professional skills.

Working with an appropriately qualified collaborator may be more effective than attempting to master everything alone. Professional authorization and confidentiality requirements must still be respected.

## 什么做法更有帮助？

_A Helpful Approach_

**让责任、能力与资源处于适当的位置。**

先确认谁适合负责什么，以及不同工作之间需要怎样交接。

协作不要求每个人承担相同份量，但应让相关人员理解自己的职责，并有机会讨论是否能够合理履行。

**Place responsibilities, capabilities, and resources where they fit best.**

Clarify who is suited to each responsibility and how the different contributions should connect.

Collaboration does not require identical workloads, but participants should understand their roles and be able to discuss whether those responsibilities are manageable.

## 什么情况需要留意？

_What to Watch For_

协作最大的风险，是参与者增加了，责任却没有更清楚。

当每个人都认为别人会处理，重要事项可能被遗漏；当所有决定仍然集中在一个人身上，所谓的共同承担也可能只是增加协调工作。

**合作如果没有清楚的角色边界，共同承担可能变成共同混乱。**

The main risk of collaboration is increasing participation without clarifying ownership.

When everyone assumes someone else will act, important tasks may be missed. When every decision still depends on one person, shared work may simply create additional coordination.

**Without clear roles and boundaries, shared responsibility can become shared confusion.**

## 现在可以做什么？

_One Small Action_

1. 选择一项需要多人参与的事务。
    
2. 列出目前由谁负责哪些部分。
    
3. 找出重复、遗漏或责任过度集中的环节。
    
4. 与相关人员讨论一项更合适的分工或交接。
    
5. 观察工作是否更顺畅、负担是否更合理。
    
6. Choose an activity involving several people.
    
7. Identify who currently handles each responsibility.
    
8. Find duplication, missing ownership, or excessive dependence on one person.
    
9. Discuss one more appropriate division or handover.
    
10. Observe whether coordination improves and responsibilities become more manageable.
    

## 留给自己的问题

_Your Reflection Question_

**哪一部分如果交给更合适的位置，你会立刻轻很多？**

**What would become lighter if it moved to a more appropriate role or position?**

---

_这张卡描述的是通过角色互补、协调与共享责任形成支持的环境条件，并不代表系统已经确认你擅长团队合作，或某个人应该替你承担责任。实际协作仍需尊重参与者的意愿、能力、权限及合理边界。_

_This card presents environmental conditions that support collaboration through complementary roles and shared responsibilities. It does not establish that you possess exceptional teamwork skills or that another person should assume your obligations. Actual collaboration requires respect for participants' willingness, capabilities, authority, and appropriate boundaries._

# F04 — 交换 | EXCHANGE

**Field · 场域条件**

**卡片副标题 | Card Subtitle**

资源、回应与价值需要保持双向流动。

Resources, response, and value need reciprocal movement.

---

## 一句话理解 | At a Glance

**真正有效的交换，不只是持续付出，而是让投入与回应之间建立合理联系，使双方能够获得有意义的价值，并支持关系或合作持续发展。**

Meaningful exchange is not simply about giving more. It creates a workable relationship between contribution and response, allowing value to move in ways that support continued cooperation.

## 这张卡想提醒什么？

_What This Card Invites You to Consider_

生活中，许多事情需要通过交换才能持续。工作需要付出与反馈，合作需要资源与责任之间的协调，家庭关系也需要适当的沟通、理解与支持。

「交换」邀请你观察：目前的投入是否获得了能够帮助事情继续发展的回应？

例如，一项服务不断增加功能，却很少了解顾客是否真正需要；一个人长期协助别人，却没有机会讨论自己的资源限制；一个团队持续提交资料，却从未收到能够改善工作的反馈。

有效交换不意味着每个人必须付出相同时间、金钱或劳动。不同角色可能具有不同责任，而有意义的回应也不一定是物质回报。

**真正重要的是，投入与回应之间存在清楚、适当且可以持续的联系。**

Many activities depend on exchange to continue. Work involves effort and feedback, cooperation requires coordinated resources and responsibilities, and family life benefits from communication, understanding, and appropriate support.

Exchange invites you to examine whether contributions receive responses that help the arrangement continue effectively.

A service may keep adding features without learning whether customers need them. Someone may provide ongoing assistance without discussing their own resource limits. A team may submit information repeatedly without receiving feedback that improves its work.

Meaningful exchange does not require everyone to contribute identical amounts of time, money, or labor. Different roles involve different responsibilities, and a useful response is not necessarily financial.

**What matters is a clear, appropriate, and sustainable connection between contribution and response.**

## 现实中可能如何出现？

_How It May Appear in Real Life_

**工作与顾客服务 | Work & Customer Service**

团队持续开发产品，但很少收集实际顾客反馈。

建立适当的反馈渠道，可以帮助团队了解哪些成果真正有价值，避免不断投入到顾客不需要的功能。

A team continues developing a product but rarely receives meaningful customer feedback.

An appropriate feedback channel can reveal which improvements create value and reduce investment in features that customers do not need.

**家庭与关系 | Family & Relationships**

家庭成员可能长期由一人承担大部分协调，其他成员却不了解实际工作量。

通过沟通需要、明确分工和提供适当支持，可以改善长期单向投入的问题。

这不代表照顾必须以等量回报交换，尤其孩子或需要照顾的人不应被要求承担不适当的责任。

One household member may handle most coordination while others remain unaware of the workload.

Discussing needs, clarifying responsibilities, and offering appropriate support can improve the arrangement.

Caregiving does not require equal repayment, particularly from children or people who depend on care.

**财务与专业合作 | Finances & Professional Cooperation**

一项专业服务可能持续投入时间与资源，却没有明确服务范围、收费或交付标准。

清楚约定双方责任、合理报酬与交付内容，可以减少误解，让合作更容易持续。

A professional service may require continuing time and resources without clearly defined scope, fees, or deliverables.

Clear agreements about responsibilities, appropriate compensation, and expected outcomes can reduce misunderstandings and support sustainable cooperation.

## 什么做法更有帮助？

_A Helpful Approach_

**检查投入与回应之间，是否具有真正有效的联系。**

先确认自己或相关人员正在提供什么，再了解对方实际需要什么，以及双方如何知道交换已经产生价值。

如果长期缺乏回应，可以考虑改善沟通、明确约定、调整投入范围，或重新评估合作方式。

**Examine whether contributions and responses are meaningfully connected.**

Clarify what is being contributed, what the other party actually needs, and how both can recognize whether the exchange is useful.

Where responses remain insufficient, consider clearer communication, agreements, revised commitments, or a different cooperative arrangement.

## 什么情况需要留意？

_What to Watch For_

交换最大的风险，是长期只有单方面投入，却缺少足以支持持续运行的回应。

另一种风险，是把所有关系都当成必须计算回报的交易。某些关系具有不对等的照顾责任，不能只用付出和获得的数量来判断。

**良好的交换重视持续性与合理回应，而不是要求每个人付出完全相同。**

The main risk is ongoing one-way contribution without sufficient response to sustain the arrangement.

Another risk is treating every relationship as a transaction requiring measurable repayment. Some relationships involve unequal caregiving obligations that cannot be evaluated through identical contributions.

**Healthy exchange values sustainability and appropriate responses rather than exact equality in what everyone gives.**

## 现在可以做什么？

_One Small Action_

1. 选择一项需要长期投入的合作或安排。
    
2. 写下双方分别投入什么、需要什么。
    
3. 找出目前缺少的反馈、沟通或资源流动。
    
4. 尝试改善一个交换环节。
    
5. 观察双方是否更容易持续参与。
    
6. Choose an arrangement requiring ongoing contributions.
    
7. Identify what each party contributes and needs.
    
8. Find a missing feedback or resource exchange.
    
9. Improve one part of the exchange.
    
10. Observe whether participation becomes more sustainable.
    

## 留给自己的问题

_Your Reflection Question_

**哪里需要的不是更多投入，而是更好的交换方式？**

**Where is the need not more effort, but a better exchange?**

---

_这张卡描述的是通过资源交换、回应与相互流动形成支持的环境条件，不代表系统已确认你正处于不公平关系、付出过多或回报不足的情况。实际交换仍须尊重自愿参与、现有责任、合理边界及相关权益。_

_This card presents environmental conditions involving reciprocal contribution, feedback, and value. It does not establish that you are in an unfair relationship or contributing more than you receive. Actual exchanges must respect voluntary participation, existing responsibilities, appropriate boundaries, and the rights of those involved._

# F05 — 节律 | RHYTHM

_What to Watch For_

支持最大的风险，是逐渐取代原本应该由相关人员承担的责任。

例如，管理者为了帮助团队，开始亲自完成所有工作；家庭成员为了避免冲突，不断替别人处理本来已经明确分配的事务。

短期内问题可能得到解决，但长期可能形成依赖，让支持者承担越来越多负荷。

**好的支持应该提升整体运行能力，而不是让所有事情更加依赖支持者。**

The main risk is allowing support to replace the responsibilities of those involved.

A manager may begin completing every task to help the team. A family member may repeatedly take over agreed responsibilities to avoid conflict.

This may resolve immediate difficulties while creating increasing dependence on the person providing support.

**Good support strengthens the overall arrangement instead of making everything more dependent on the supporter.**

## 现在可以做什么？

_One Small Action_

1. 选择一件目前需要额外努力才能完成的事情。
    
2. 找出最主要的阻碍条件。
    
3. 确认需要什么工具、资源、权限或协助。
    
4. 尝试改善其中一个条件。
    
5. 观察工作是否变得更容易，而不是仅由别人代为完成。
    
6. Choose an activity that currently requires repeated extra effort.
    
7. Identify its main limiting condition.
    
8. Determine what tool, resource, permission, or assistance is needed.
    
9. Improve one supporting condition.
    
10. Observe whether the task becomes easier without simply transferring all responsibility to someone else.
    

## 留给自己的问题

_Your Reflection Question_

**什么条件一旦存在，你会明显不需要那么用力？**

**Which condition, if present, would noticeably reduce how hard you need to push?**

Health Reality

Organize what you are experiencing, what evidence exists, what remains unknown, and what may need professional attention.

Important boundary

This surface does not diagnose conditions, rule out serious disease, prescribe treatment, or replace a clinician. Urgent symptoms require appropriate real-world medical care.

Record an observation

No network health-fact retrieval occurs without explicit processing consent. Health facts require approved current authority. Persistent health records remain disabled.

What this runtime is designed to keep separate

User-reported observations, home or device measurements, laboratory or imaging evidence, clinician findings, and unknowns remain distinct evidence classes. Symbolic methods are not medical evidence.

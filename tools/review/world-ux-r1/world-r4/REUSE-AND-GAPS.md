## Official sources actually checked

* DOSM: four original CSV downloads plus official metadata pages, exact SHA256 and row positions. CC BY4.0 with attribution; publisher is DOSM, no endorsement. Data observed through 2026-09; metadata last update 2026-10-19 13:12 MYT; retrieved2026-10-23. Separate publication date is not fabricated.
* DOSM MCOICOP dictionary: native two-digit codes retained with official English/Malay labels. Chinese customer labels are editorial UI candidates, not accepted book glossary changes.
* BNM: official portal disclosed its specification endpoints and Accept version. Actual public OPR, loan-applied and loan-approved paths returned200 without credentials. This does not prove every BNM endpoint is public or licensed for redistribution. Inspected mortgage fields do not establish matched application/result counts; units were not established in the inspected schema. No approval-rate ratio is calculated.
* MET: data.gov.my forecast returned a real Langkawi record; it is forecast, not measured rain, hydrology or crop output. Direct MET API documentation has a different access path/authentication policy. No key was obtained or bypassed.
* MyGDI: catalogue/request procedure accessible; this is not a granted geospatial layer licence. No watershed layer or rainfall→agriculture geographic join is claimed.
* World Bank: existing population/GDP identifiers checked against actual 2025 Malaysia responses. Annual economic background stays separate from monthly CPI. General data terms allow reuse with attribution subject to indicator exceptions; no blanket licence claim for all indicators.

Jurisdiction crosswalk for the two selected states follows the official state-code page: https://sdk.myinvois.hasil.gov.my/codes/state-codes/ (Johor01, Selangor10). Native DOSM geography identifiers remain attached; no geometry/boundary precision is inferred.

## Real versus synthetic evidence

Real: raw public data, CSV measurements, source metadata, readout, state comparison, observed sequence and source objects.

Synthetic: QA account identities, household question, applicability statements, negative modified copies (missing values, wrong method/version, mismatched period). They test engineering boundaries and are not new observations or evidence of a customer outcome.

Selecting August instead of September in a QA Case is a user-selected source-period change. It is not claimed to be a same-period publisher correction or a change in that household's life.

## Scope limits and next actions

1. C01–C14 coverage is in the existing registry successor. Only CPI measurement coverage is newly local-observable; population/GDP reuse an existing background owner. Forecast reachability is not measured climate/water coverage.
2. CaseA answers price observations and category rate differences, not a causal decomposition of cost pressure. Household income, expenditure weights and bills are missing. Category YoY rates are not contributions to total inflation.
3. CaseB cannot establish rainfall/water/food-supply impacts without measured rain, catchment/storage and output series with aligned geography/periods/licences.
4. CaseC cannot establish approval difficulty without matched application counts, approval/rejection definitions, cohort/product/institution coverage and comparable history. Amount fields, house prices and OPR are not substitutes. Reading APIs successfully does not close these gaps.
5. General membership synthesis, personal navigation quality, production data freshness operations and commercial delivery are NOT_VERIFIED by this work.
6. Current source datasets are local pinned files under `.tmp/world-r4/`. Production activation needs a reviewed delivery/cache/source-revalidation adapter and permission/configuration decision; do not deploy an isolated Node/SQLite server, QA cookie or filesystem path as a production handler.

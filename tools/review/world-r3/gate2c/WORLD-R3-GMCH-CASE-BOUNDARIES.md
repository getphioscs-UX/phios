# Three contrasting cases
Status PARTIAL: mechanism comparison, not common-cause identification.

| Case | Time / actual source | What can be discussed | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| US subprime/housing crisis | 2007–2010; [Federal Reserve History](https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/subprime-mortgage-crisis), official historical narrative read | Housing lending, securitization, losses and financial disruption interacted | All mortgage slowdowns cause crisis; this is current2026 data; named author's paper wholesale republication |
| Japan post-bubble | Late1980s asset boom,1990s/2002 bank-asset burden; [BOJ2002 discussion](https://www2.boj.or.jp/archive/en/announcements/press/koen_2002/ko0212b.htm) full official article read; numeric timeline comparison still PARTIAL | Need distinguish bubble legacy from newly arising industrial/corporate distress | Samecause as US; interest rates alone explain it; quantitative magnitudes not reproduced |
| China property adjustment | Calendar2025 observed, NBS published2026-01-20; [official release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202601/t20260120_1962353.html) read | Developer funds, sales, construction and investment are different measures | Developer personalmortgage receipts are application rejections;2025data are2026outcomes; crisis prediction |

Alternative explanations must remain visible: fewer willing borrowers with unchanged standards (actual2024Q3 governmentmortgage SLOOS example); lower rates with expensive homes/low incomes; slower mortgages with tight rentals; weak property alongside growth in other sectors; reporting changes generating breaks; coincident indicators without identified causal effect.

Fresh numeric local trends, matched cohorts, complete historical timelines and case-specific transmission estimates are not closed by this design. No universal housing leading-indicator claim.

# Comparability rules
APPROVAL COUNT ≠ APPROVAL RATE. Sameperiod approvedamount/appliedamount is only a monetary flowratio, may exceed100%, never applicantprobability. Require sameapplicationcohort, purchase/refinance, peoplevsrecords, lendercoverage and exclusions.

SURVEY NET SHARES ≠ REJECTIONS. Tightened minus eased differs from stronger minus weaker demand; weights/sample and questionnaireperiod mandatory. STOCK ≠ DISBURSEMENT ≠ DEMAND. CNdeveloperreceipts, UKnetapprovals, MYamountflows and USreportableannualactions cannot enter a singleapprovalratechart.

Policy instruments OPR/FOMCrange/DFR/BankRate/BOJframework/PBOCoperations-LPR remain labelled; observed MYOR/EFFR/ESTR/callrate are marketseries. No globaluniformovernightrate.

Chart requires match on metricdefinition, unit, frequency, seasonality, period, geography, population, dwelling/loan type, currency, inflationadjustment and revision. Daily/monthly/annual side-by-side labelled only, not blended. Ifresampling authorised: showmethod (endperiodstock, sumflow, appropriate weightedaverage rates); no forwardfill fromfuture.

BISDSR compares ownhistory preferentially; modelledaverage maturities, lendingrates and incomeproxies prevent simplistic crosscountryabsolute ranking. Houseprice indices retain qualitymethod/base/type/area; rebase doesnot erasecoverage differences.

Deliberate counterexamples and executable rejected-claim tests in validate-contract.mjs. All synthetic counterexample numbers are testfixtures, never observations. Unknown, missing and zero must remain distinct.

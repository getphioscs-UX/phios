这些资料怎样帮助理解你How these records help you understand your situation

工作地点变化与本周负荷增加出现在同一组本人陈述中。这提供了理解本周处境的两个线索，但没有证明地点变化导致负荷增加，也没有证明这是长期模式。
A location change and a heavier work week appear in the same set of your statements. They offer two clues to this week’s situation, without proving that the move caused the workload or that either is a lasting pattern.

可以分别观察新地点的通勤或工作条件，以及任务量、休息和恢复的变化。这些是核对线索，不是已记录事实。
You could observe the new location’s travel or working conditions separately from task volume, rest and recovery. These are prompts for checking the clues, not recorded facts.

下一次记录什么会更有用What would make the next observation useful

工作现场观察仍然缺失。可以记录一个具体情境、你当时做了什么、发生了什么变化，以及后来是否重复；若保留评估结果，再补充量表名称和日期。尚未记录的关系、财务和长期适合度保持未知。
Workplace observations are still missing. A useful next record would name a concrete situation, what you did, what changed and whether it happened again; for an assessment, add its instrument and date. Unrecorded relationship, financial and long-term suitability information remains unknown.

Assessments are optional. Saving requires selection and consent. / 评估可选。保存需要明确选择与同意。

# PHI OS Canonical Article Production Brief

This template is rendered only by `knowledge:export-brief`. Tokens are populated
from authoritative Registry, Blueprint, Production Readiness and Governance
records. A rendered brief is a controlled input snapshot, not a new source of
truth and not publication authority.

Required sections:

1. Brief Identity
2. Canonical Node Identity
3. Localized Identity
4. Canonical Thesis
5. Article Boundary
6. Editorial Contract
7. Structured Article Contract
8. Claim Governance
9. Source Governance
10. Review Governance
11. Node-specific Inputs
12. Package Output Contract
13. Forbidden Actions

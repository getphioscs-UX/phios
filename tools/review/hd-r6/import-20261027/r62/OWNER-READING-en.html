7.2 Six Lines, Different Ways of Expressing a Theme

Every Gate contains six Lines, each offering a finer distinction within the broader theme of that Gate. Understanding these differences allows the reading to become more precise without turning a person's identity into a fixed formula.

A careful interpretation keeps these relationships visible. What distinguishes one chart from another is not simply the number of activated Gates, but how those activations are positioned and connected.

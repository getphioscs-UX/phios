## 1.4 How to Use the Reading

Read Type, Authority and Definition together before concentrating on individual Centers or activations. When an interpretation seems relevant, connect it with a specific situation: what choice was available, what conditions were known, and what actually happened? If the description does not fit, retain that difference rather than reshaping your experience to protect the chart. This private reading uses a symbolic framework, and certain advanced variables and Line details retain calculation limitations. Its most useful role is to help you ask clearer questions while leaving the meaning and direction of your life with you.

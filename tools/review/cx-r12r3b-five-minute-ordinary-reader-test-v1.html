Five-Minute Ordinary Reader Test

Give the reviewer the live customer result without internal terminology assistance. Time limit: 5 minutes.

  1. What is this graph showing?
  2. What are the three most important points?
  3. Why does the system consider them worth noticing?
  4. What does the system still not know?
  5. Have the methods been mixed into one conclusion?
  6. What can you do next?

Pass only if the reviewer can answer all six without developer/internal terminology assistance.

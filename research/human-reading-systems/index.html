PHI OS keeps historical, psychological, spiritual, professional, search and AI approaches inside explicit authority boundaries.

Study what each method notices—and what it cannot establish.

Historical reading methods offer structured ways to discuss patterns, timing and relationships. Comparing them requires attention to their assumptions and differences, not a claim that they all measure the same thing. Their interpretations should remain distinct from independently checked facts and from qualified professional advice.

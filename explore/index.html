FOUR QUESTIONS

What is PHI OS, why does it exist, how is it different, and where do I start?

01

What is PHI OS?

A Reality Navigation platform: one place to understand a changing situation, draw on relevant knowledge or perspectives, choose a bounded next step and return to what actually changed.

02

Why does it exist?

Because more answers do not automatically create orientation. Information, interpretation, constraints, decisions and outcomes often remain separated.

Why PHI OS →
03

How is it different?

It does not treat every source as the same kind of evidence or every question as a chatbot prompt. Knowledge, personal context, symbolic perspectives, self-report and professional evidence keep their boundaries.

04

Where do I start?

Start from your intent: ask a question, look through another perspective, work through a real situation, seek professional help or learn the framework.

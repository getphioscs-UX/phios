PHI OS brings knowledge, personal perspectives and your chosen real-life context together. Read, compare, choose a bounded action, and return to what changed.

Why PHI OS?

The parts of a life do not change separately.

A change in work can affect money, relationships and the time available to recover. Advice aimed at only one part may miss the cost elsewhere. PHI OS helps you ask how these parts relate, while keeping facts, interpretations and open questions distinct.

Understand → Read → Navigate → Act → Review → Continue

  1. 01Understand

    Clarify the situation, question or context before reaching for an answer.

  2. 02Read

    Bring in the knowledge or perspective that is actually relevant to this situation.

  3. 03Navigate

    See possible directions, trade-offs, dependencies, risks and what remains uncertain.

  4. 04Act

    Choose a bounded next step rather than pretending the system can decide your life for you.

  5. 05Review

    Compare what you expected with what actually happened and what new evidence appeared.

  6. 06Continue

    Update your understanding as reality changes instead of treating one reading as final.

For example, describe a change in your work, read the relevant source, choose a small reversible action, then record what actually happened. A professional question may require a qualified person.

A few useful distinctions

Do I need Profile before a reading?

No. It is optional and independent.

Does a reading decide what I should do?

No. It is a perspective to compare with experience, not a guaranteed prediction.

Is anything saved automatically?

Use the save and consent controls in each actual workspace. This explanation page stores no personal input.

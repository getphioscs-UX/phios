# PDS-W9｜Knowledge 与 Professional 页面

Baseline: `main@4f074b116f29ec7346f922cc44a2aa93666e1920`.

W9 adds one shared presentation contract to 26 existing pages. It aligns focus
visibility, 44px controls, form behaviour, card surfaces, responsive overflow,
reduced motion and increased contrast without moving, hiding or regenerating
page content.

The three-book/fourteen-part Knowledge architecture, Product and Professional
Service separation, membership entitlements, payment states, Professional
Consent and Responsibility, External Reader boundaries, Privacy, Terms and AI
Disclosure remain owned by their existing registries, contracts and scripts.

Run `npm run check:pds-w9` to verify page coverage and SHA-256 hashes for the
frozen Knowledge, commerce and Professional artifacts.

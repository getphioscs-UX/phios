# PWS-W1A Preparation — Revenue and Modality Extension

## Purpose

This extension keeps the frozen M4A-W1 professional-service registries intact and adds a higher-level revenue architecture. Knowledge products, human professional services, regulated planning and experiential modalities are no longer treated as the same commercial object.

## Four contract distinctions

1. A book, audio, video or course purchase creates an entitlement, not a professional relationship.
2. A human interpretation or facilitated session creates a service only after scope, consent, eligibility and assignment are valid.
3. Financial, insurance and investment planning require jurisdiction, qualification, suitability and disclosure gates.
4. Human Design, I Ching, Tarot, sandtray, guided consciousness exploration and restorative sessions remain interpretation, symbolic material or subjective experience; they do not become objective evidence automatically.

## Revenue families

- Knowledge products: books, audio, video, courses and workshops.
- Runtime professional services: professional reading, consultation, navigation design and follow-up.
- Interpretive readers: Human Design, I Ching and Tarot.
- Experiential reconstruction: sandtray, deep guided consciousness exploration and restorative Reiki sessions.
- Regulated financial planning: financial, insurance and investment planning.

## Runtime rule

Every material statement in an integrated service must carry a source class, modality, limitation and confirmation status. Symbolic projection may create a question; subjective experience may create a review signal; neither proves historical fact, diagnosis or investment suitability.

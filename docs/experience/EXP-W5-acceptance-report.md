## Customer experience decision

Reality Reading now presents the customer result before evidence mechanics or technical structure. The default Customer View follows one explicit sequence: current understanding, core change, why it may continue, confirmed information, unknowns, and what is worth noticing next.

Supporting basis, related conditions, conflicts, judgement limits and another possible explanation are in an optional second layer. Coordinates, Capability, Interfaces, Figure references, source lineage, schemas, state and version remain in closed Evidence/Technical views or the later technical disclosure. The complete technical model now occurs after the customer Reading and its actions in document order.

Direct customer statements, confirmed facts, system associations, interpretations, unknowns and conflicts use distinct labels, colour accents and semantic states. The primary action confirms the current Reading and continues through the existing Navigation gate. A separate action explicitly returns to Reconstruction when content is inaccurate.

The empty state now explains that Reading is not ready and provides the recovery route to Reconstruction.

## Required Work Package sequence

1. **EXP-W1 — Global IA and shared-shell contract alignment**: resolve E01-E02 before any page work, because every route inherits the conflict.
2. **EXP-W2 — Discover/Home and About first-time orientation**: establish one entry decision and one plain category explanation.
3. **EXP-W3 — Journey Overview, Demo and Entry production experience**: freeze one visible stage model and one dominant start path.
4. **EXP-W4 — Reconstruction customer confirmation experience**: make formation confirmation primary and move operator/technical material out of default view.
5. **EXP-W5 — Reading customer product experience**: expose the customer map before research and technical models.
6. **EXP-W6 — Navigation, Review, Memory and Continuity task separation**: restore one task per page/state and plain recovery language.
7. **EXP-W7 — Knowledge route consolidation**: define Hub/Atlas/Book/Thesis/Figure relationships and remove looping summaries.
8. **EXP-W8 — Professional service decision experience**: provide coherent offers, deliverables, price, turnaround, availability and deliberate handoff.
9. **EXP-W9 — Customer-language and duplicate-content closure**: bilingual content pass after structures are stable.
10. **EXP-W10 — Production experience acceptance**: repeat the five tasks, both locales and required breakpoints; automation remains regression evidence only.

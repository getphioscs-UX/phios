## Route and responsibility decision

`/` is the only Home URL and `Discover` is its navigation label. No second Discover page exists. Home now performs first-time orientation; About explains purpose, design responsibility, limits and builder trust without repeating the product catalogue.

## First-time user answers

1. PHI OS is a Reality Navigation platform for keeping a changing situation, its evidence, uncertainty and next steps connected.
2. It helps a person describe change, understand how it formed, choose a bounded direction and review what actually changed.
3. It is not a general chatbot because it does not treat the latest prompt as the whole situation or turn interpretation into fact.
4. The primary next step is to understand the Reality Journey; free Demo, free Knowledge and Professional services are explicitly secondary.
5. Demo and public Knowledge are labelled free; the personal Journey is separate; Professional services are separately paid and require scope, consent and human responsibility.

## System Position

Preface is the pilot scope, not the system boundary.

The B1 Contract applies to every registered Canonical Knowledge Node, every
supported Book and Part, and each locale-specific Readiness Record. A future
registered Node enters the same content-population assessment without changing
the Exporter, Validator or Importer. Registry and Blueprint remain the identity
and planning authorities; Blueprint-only Nodes do not become Canonical
identities.

The current execution batch is limited to `KN-PREFACE-002–013`. It does not
register Part 1–14 Nodes and does not turn planned Blueprint Nodes into
production obligations.

## Universal Content Population Contract

Every registered Canonical Node must complete the following governed decisions:

1. Canonical Thesis: proposition, necessity, mechanism, system role and
   continuity.
2. Article Boundary: Must Establish, Required Distinctions, Must Not Claim,
   included scope, excluded scope, entry, completion and handoff.
3. Claim Boundary: internal framework claims, externally verifiable claims,
   qualification and prohibited claims.
4. Supporting Question Treatment: one canonical owner and a controlled
   treatment value for every mapped question.
5. Source Plan: internal Canonical sources, external verification requirements,
   preferred and prohibited source types, gaps and citation sensitivity.
6. Figure Decision: `none`, `optional` or `required`, with Media Brief before
   Asset Registry and Article Figure eligibility.
7. Public Boundary: Public Knowledge, paid Book depth, Runtime guidance,
   Professional interpretation, enterprise and developer boundaries.
8. Editorial Contract: article purpose, audience, language policy, continuity,
   completion boundary, AI authority and human approval boundary.
9. Human Editorial Freeze: named human reviewer, timestamp and explicit
   approval after all blocking findings are closed.

The node-specific Editorial Contract is represented through the existing
Readiness fields and the frozen PJA-W2A authority:

| Editorial requirement | Existing authority |
| --- | --- |
| Article purpose | Canonical Thesis and Must Establish |
| Audience | PJA-W2A public knowledge boundary |
| Language policy | Locale and Localization Readiness |
| Canonical continuity | Sequence Boundary |
| Completion boundary | Included and Excluded Scope |
| AI authority boundary | PJA-W2A frozen AI authority rules |
| Human approval boundary | Review and Human Editorial Freeze |

No duplicate Editorial Schema or second Canonical Thesis source is created.

## Authority Boundary

Tools may load authority, validate structure, detect omissions, verify ownership,
check continuity, reject duplicates and report blocking reasons.

Tools must not create a Canonical Thesis, decide a Node boundary, approve
claims, invent sources, select a Figure decision, sign Human Editorial Freeze,
or promote a record to `production_ready`.

Schema validity does not constitute Canonical approval. Content completeness
does not constitute human review. Production readiness does not constitute
article approval, publication readiness or publication.

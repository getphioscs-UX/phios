Heading / 标题

First emphasis: learning, support and absorption

Opening / 开篇

These themes have first priority in the capability reading. This priority is not a measurement of your personality.

Meaning / 意义

Capability is considered through what can be absorbed, practised, expressed and carried repeatedly. These dimensions coexist rather than forming a sequence.

Associated themes include peer relationships and self-position, alongside rules, responsibility and pressure. They remain secondary to the first emphasis.

The structural link between self-position and expression provides context for the reading, without establishing any real-life effect.

Separately, the structural tension between environment and self-position also provides context, without establishing any real-life effect.

A final strong-or-weak judgement remains open and should not be treated as a fixed identity.

Tension / 张力

Support, expression and external demands need to be considered together. The reading does not establish how you experience them.

Closing / 收束

Keep learning, support and absorption as the reading’s first emphasis—not as a measured personality trait.

Observation / 观察

Which of these themes fits a concrete experience, and which does not?

Counterexample / 反例

What in your experience does not fit this reading?

Local scope / 局部范围

This is a conditional symbolic reading, not a statement of observed behaviour or a prediction of events.

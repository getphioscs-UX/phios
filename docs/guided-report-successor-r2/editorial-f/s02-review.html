Heading / 标题

Learning, support and absorption take first emphasis

Opening / 开篇

Learning, support and absorption are the main reading priority here. This priority is not a measurement of your personality or ability.

Meaning / 意义

Capability is considered through four dimensions at once: what can be absorbed, practiced, expressed and carried consistently.

Peer relationships and self-position, along with rules, responsibility and pressure, are secondary associated themes; they do not replace the first emphasis.

The structural link between self-position and expression forms part of the reading context.

Separately, the structural tension between environment and self-position also forms part of the reading context.

A final strong-or-weak judgment remains open and should not be treated as a fixed identity.

Tension / 张力

Support, expression and external demands need to be considered together, while how they are experienced remains undetermined.

Closing / 收束

Absorbing, practicing, expressing and carrying something consistently are considered together as simultaneous dimensions, not as a sequence.

Observation / 观察

Counterexample / 反例

Local scope / 局部范围

This is a conditional symbolic reading, not a statement about observed behavior or a prediction of events.

首要关注：学习、支持与吸收

Opening / 开篇

在能力主题中，学习、支持与吸收是首要解读重点；这表示阅读上的优先顺序，不是测得的人格特质。

Meaning / 意义

能力可从吸收、练习、表达与反复承载几个同时存在的维度来理解。

次要相关主题包括同类关系与自我位置，以及规则、责任与压力；它们不取代首要重点。

自我位置与表达之间的结构关系，是理解这一主题时保留的一项背景。

环境与自我位置之间的结构关系，是另一项需要单独保留的背景。

最终强弱判断仍保留，不据此定义固定身份。

Tension / 张力

支持、表达与外部要求需要放在一起考虑；命盘不能证明它们在现实中如何被体验。

Closing / 收束

吸收、练习、表达与反复承载是同时考虑的维度，而不是先后发生的步骤。

Observation / 观察

这些主题中，哪些符合你的一段具体经历，哪些并不符合？

Counterexample / 反例

你的经历中，有哪些部分并不符合这份解读？

Local scope / 局部范围

这是一份有条件的象征性解读，不代表已经观察到的行为，也不预测事件。

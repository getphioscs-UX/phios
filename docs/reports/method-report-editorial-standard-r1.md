# Method report editorial contract R1 — local candidate

The Owner-supplied Numerology R2.1 manuscript is editorial guidance and one frozen synthetic replay, not an interpretation authority or a reusable answer for other birth inputs.

## Shared contract

Method-specific trusted calculations and exact eligible role/value/school claims → one bilingual semantic plan → bounded prose → structural and semantic evaluation → immutable editorial snapshot → existing publication and PWS/private-delivery owners.

Implementation: `functions/personal-reading/narrative/method-editorial-contract.js`. NUM source adapter: `numerology-editorial-adapter.js`. Other methods are not connected or activated by this change. Their own page counts, calculation owners, interpretation rights and accepted visuals remain authoritative.

## Allowed editorial freedom

Use coherent paragraphs, conditions, explicit hypothetical everyday illustrations, counterexamples and optional reversible observation questions. Preserve every calculation and the exact scope of the tradition. A role definition is not a value-specific meaning. No fabricated events, partner, health, wealth, destiny or professional conclusions. Preserve matching meanings, unknowns and exceptions in Chinese and English. Audit IDs stay outside customer prose.

## What automatic checks establish

Frozen request identity, eligible source identities, language/unit presence, claim-ID and scope validity, mapped paragraphs, illustration metadata, obvious certainty/jargon risks, exact source/fact snapshot identity and the integer USD2 whole-order ceiling calculation.

They do **not** prove semantic entailment, correct attribution of every sentence, every negation/qualification, fluent translation or usefulness. ID-valid prose can still be wrong. Numeric associations also need semantic evaluation; a number appearing in the allowed facts is not sufficient proof of its use in a sentence. No production semantic guarantee or per-answer manual customer approval flow is introduced.

## Lifecycle and privacy

Semantic, editorial and publication snapshots are distinct. Request identity includes account, entitlement, confirmed inputs, source identities and prompt version. Only local mock/replay adapters are enabled. The single-runner in-flight map is local coalescing, **not** a distributed generation lock. Production orchestration must use the existing PWS leased job and atomic private persistence; do not expose this helper as a new API. Unknown execution/cost must reconcile before another attempt. Reopening and re-rendering reuse saved prose; they do not request a new model answer.

The local NUM adapter persists the editorial replay under the existing PRIVATE_REPORTS interface and validates its content digest. It does not register NUM for production or grant real entitlements. Isolated paid-state fixtures are not Stripe Webhook evidence.

## Cost and remaining gate

USD2 covers the entire NUM order, bilingual content, repairs and all four included follow-ups. Use verified model prices and tokenization, integer micro-USD, existing cost reservation/settlement owners and zero automatic retries. This round verifies budget arithmetic with synthetic prices only. Live model choice, actual token plan, cost reservation integration and model semantic-quality evaluation remain unverified. Never infer affordability from the mock result.

The proposed 90/100 editorial rubric is a review target, not a release switch. Calculation/source acceptance, editorial acceptance, layout proof, private QA and production authorization remain separate.

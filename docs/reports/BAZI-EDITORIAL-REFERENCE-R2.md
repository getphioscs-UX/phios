## Human-accepted benchmark

GEN-01 Section 1 — 核心性格与能力 — HUMAN ACCEPTED.

The accepted section establishes the customer-facing BaZi standard:
- write a personal reading, not an essay or methodology memo;
- each section answers only its own main question;
- do not pre-consume Career, Wealth, Relationships, Family, Timing or Navigation content;
- keep BaZi technical evidence visible enough to ground the interpretation, but translate it into lived meaning rather than a symbol dictionary;
- prefer specific interpretation of thinking, judgment, learning, expression and capability formation over generic advice;
- avoid repeated “how to verify / how to say / cannot say” governance prose;
- do not invent biography, strength/weakness, useful-god, transformation or unestablished pattern conclusions;
- professional boundaries remain internal to authoring/QA and are not rendered as a customer-facing “专业附注” block;
- target density is section-sized: complete enough to feel professional, concise enough to leave later sections with distinct work to do.

## Section ownership contract

### Section 1 — 核心性格与能力
Owns:
- thinking and judgment style
- learning and knowledge absorption
- expression style
- how capability is formed and used
- internal decision logic directly relevant to personality/capability

Must not expand into:
- career fit or occupational prescription
- wealth strategy or income conclusions
- relationship dynamics beyond incidental illustration
- family roles
- timing forecasts
- whole-life guidance

### Section 2 — 人生格局与发展主轴
Owns:
- whole-chart organization and dominant developmental axes
- interaction among established stems/branches, ten-god themes and chart relationships
- long-range structural emphasis without claiming unestablished 格局/强弱/用神
- what kinds of tensions or integrations repeatedly organize development

Must not repeat:
- the detailed personality/capability mechanics already owned by Section 1
- career-specific role/industry discussion
- wealth-specific resource strategy
- relationship-specific interpretation
- timing-specific predictions

## Reuse rule

Future BaZi reports should reproduce the editorial quality and section discipline of this reference, not copy GEN-01 subject-specific conclusions or wording. Subject semantics must always come from that subject’s governed Authoring/Authority Pack.

## Acceptance rule

A later section is not editorially accepted merely because it passes machine checks. Human ACCEPT remains section-specific.

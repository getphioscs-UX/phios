## Frozen purpose

This phase freezes the knowledge-production boundary and the Book I knowledge
blueprint. It does not continue expanding the Knowledge Registry.
It does not become a prerequisite or blocking gate for Professional Workspace,
Payment, Journey or customer delivery development.

## Completed foundation

KH-W3.5A–D completed seven deliverables:

1. Source Inventory;
2. Knowledge Node Inventory;
3. Relationship Audit;
4. Inventory Freeze;
5. PKR Canonical Data Model;
6. Content Production and ChatGPT Instruction; and
7. Preface Registry Population.

Their status remains Completed. Where node granularity or Preface population
changed, KH-W3.5E is now canonical and the earlier population remains
historical evidence rather than a competing source of truth.

## KH-W3.5E consolidation

The provisional 36 Preface questions were consolidated into 13 Canonical Knowledge Nodes and 23 Supporting Questions.
All 36 questions remain represented and no duplicate question was deleted.

The canonical rules are:

- one page is not one node;
- one heading is not one node;
- one question is not automatically one article; and
- only an independent reusable mechanism may become a Canonical Knowledge
  Node.

## KH-W3.5G Book I boundary

KH-W3.5G owns the Book I planning blueprint:

- 13 Preface Canonical Nodes;
- 65 planned nodes beyond the Preface;
- 78 planned Canonical Nodes in total; and
- no more than 8 simultaneously active first-round articles.

Blueprint presence does not require immediate production. Articles do not
replace Book I, and Supporting Questions do not become articles
automatically.

## Non-blocking boundary

This step adds no Knowledge Registry level, node, Supporting Question, public
page, article, Runtime operation, business object or Migration. It does not
modify Professional Workspace, Payment, Journey, Provider, Assignment,
Deliverable or customer delivery behaviour.

Future content production must run as a separate authorised step. Any future
Registry expansion requires an explicit contract version upgrade and cannot be
introduced silently through a page, article draft or ChatGPT production task.

## PDS acceptance

There is no customer-visible implementation in this step. The 360px, 768px
and 1440px visual checks, Chinese and English checks, keyboard/focus checks and
touch target checks are therefore not applicable. Runtime regression remains
mandatory. Production verification can be signed only after the delta is
committed and deployed.

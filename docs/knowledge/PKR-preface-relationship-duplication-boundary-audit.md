# PKR Preface Relationship, Duplication & Boundary Audit

Contract: `PHI-OS-PKR-Preface-Audit-v1.1.0`

## Decision

The previous 36-item population was a provisional question inventory, not an acceptable long-term canonical-node model. This audit freezes mechanism-based granularity. Page count must never determine node count.

## Result

- Previous formal nodes: 36
- Canonical knowledge nodes: 13
- Supporting questions: 23
- Preserved questions: 36 of 36
- Canonical themes/domains: 6
- Independent article reduction: approximately 64%
- Duplicate deletion: 0

## Boundary rules

A canonical node must carry an independent mechanism, support a non-duplicative article, maintain a clear boundary, and justify long-term bilingual maintenance. A supporting question may be embedded in an article, surfaced through search, or used as a FAQ entry, but does not require an independent article. Search aliases are retrieval expressions only.

## Scale rule for future parts

A part of approximately 100 pages should normally produce 12–20 canonical nodes, not one node per page or one node per question. Supporting questions may be numerous without producing separate assets.

## Status

Approved. The v1.0.1 36-node population is superseded by v1.1.0.

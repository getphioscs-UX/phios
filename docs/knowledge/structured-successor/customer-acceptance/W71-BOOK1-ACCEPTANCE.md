It records reader identity, language, deployment URL, elapsed time and evidence for each task. Page references alone do not pass source access; an Ask link alone does not pass a completed question. Observer help should be recorded.

The packet binds the current Book I registry and loading/search/Ask policy source hashes. Exported records are review drafts only. Five passes within 300 seconds make a draft eligible for review, never automatically accepted. A stale digest, missing evidence or reader identity, duplicate/missing tasks, incomplete timing or any failed task prevents eligibility. No decision is imported or applied by this stage.

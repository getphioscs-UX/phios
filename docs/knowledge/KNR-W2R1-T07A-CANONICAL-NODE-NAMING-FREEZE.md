## Naming contract

1. Each title describes one mechanism rather than a broad topic.
2. Prefer natural `为什么……` or `……如何……` constructions when they improve comprehension.
3. The mechanism should be understandable on first reading.
4. Avoid literary or translation-like phrasing.
5. Keep titles stable and naturally translatable for English production, search, Knowledge Graph and LLM retrieval.

3. Prepare the Part Mapping Review again.
4. Do not rebuild or delete confirmed P0 Mapping ranges or hashes.
5. If P0 display titles are consumed from a persisted record outside the Blueprint, handle them through a title-only migration; do not remap manuscript ranges.

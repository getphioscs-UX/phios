Wealth Outlook

Wealth Outlook reads the chart’s relationship with resources: how value is produced, exchanged, directed, maintained and placed under responsibility. In BaZi, “wealth” is first a structural function. Actual income, assets and financial outcomes still depend on real-world decisions and conditions outside the chart.

Key Insights

  1. 01

    Wealth is read as a resource cycle: how value enters, what must be exchanged for it and what competing demands act on it afterward.

  2. 02

    Resource opportunity and resource retention are different structural questions. Receiving more does not automatically mean keeping more.

  3. 03

    Symbolic wealth structure does not equal a financial outcome. Real decisions still require cash flow, obligations, risk and market evidence outside the chart.

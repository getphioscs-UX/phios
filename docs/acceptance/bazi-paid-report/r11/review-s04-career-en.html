Career Development

Career Development reads how the chart handles responsibility, output, standards, resources and role boundaries. Rather than assigning a single “best profession,” this chapter looks at the kinds of working conditions in which the chart’s functions can be used coherently and sustained over time.

Key Insights

  1. 01

    Career fit depends more on role structure than on job title: responsibility, autonomy, standards, resources and support need to be read together.

  2. 02

    The same capability can operate very differently under different working conditions. Authority, workload and clarity of expectations materially change how the structure is carried.

  3. 03

    Long-term career direction is best read from themes that repeat across several parts of the chart, not from one isolated profession symbol.

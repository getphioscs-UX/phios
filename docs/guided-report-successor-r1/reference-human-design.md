**A birth-data-based system that organizes Type, decision mechanics, Centers, Channels, and structural differences into a personal design map.**

### 中文固定正文

人类图是一套现代个人结构读取体系，它根据出生日期、时间与地点生成一张个人图表，并通过类型、策略、内在权威、人生角色、定义方式、中心、通道、闸门以及部分进阶变量来描述不同结构之间的差异。

它不是只回答“你是什么类型”。类型与策略提供较高层级的运行入口，内在权威聚焦决策方式，中心与通道描述较稳定或较开放的结构关系，而人生角色、定义与进阶变量则进一步增加个体差异。完整读取的重点因此不是把所有术语逐项列出来，而是理解这些部分怎样共同形成一套设计结构。

人类图提供的是一种解释框架，而不是经过医学或生物学测量得到的人体事实。图表结果可以作为自我观察的一种视角，但不能仅凭图表就证明一个人的健康、能力、人格事实或未来结果。

### Fixed English Copy

Human Design is a modern personal-structure system that generates a chart from birth date, time, and location. It organizes structural differences through elements such as Type, Strategy, Inner Authority, Profile, Definition, Centers, Channels, Gates, and—where available—advanced Variables.

It is not simply a system for answering “What Type are you?” Type and Strategy provide a high-level operating framework, Inner Authority focuses on decision mechanics, Centers and Channels describe more consistent or open structural relationships, while Profile, Definition, and advanced Variables add further differentiation. A complete reading therefore focuses on how these components work together rather than presenting every technical item as an isolated definition.

Human Design is an interpretive framework, not a medical or biological measurement of the body. Its chart can offer a perspective for self-observation, but the chart alone does not establish a person's health, capability, personality as scientific fact, or future outcome.

人类图并不是一套从古代连续传承至今的单一传统，而是在二十世纪末形成的现代综合体系。它把来自占星、易经、脉轮／中心概念以及其他象征与结构语言重新组织在同一张图表中，并建立类型、策略、内在权威、中心、通道、闸门、人生角色与变量等自己的术语和读取逻辑。

这意味着，人类图中使用的一些组成元素拥有更早的文化来源，但“Human Design”作为一个完整体系本身是现代形成的。它后来的传播依赖教学、图表软件、专业读取与不断扩大的使用者社群，因此今天不同实践者之间也可能在解释深度、用语和应用方式上出现差异。

理解这种历史位置很重要。它可以帮助我们区分哪些部分属于 Human Design 自己建立的结构，哪些概念借用了更早的传统语言，以及哪些后续解释来自现代实践。体系的流行和持续传播说明它具有显著的文化与自我观察价值，但并不因此自动把所有理论主张转化为现代科学事实。

### Fixed English Copy

Human Design is not a single ancient tradition transmitted continuously into the present. It is a modern synthesis that emerged in the late twentieth century, reorganizing concepts associated with astrology, the I Ching, center-based models, and other symbolic or structural languages into a new chart system with its own terminology and reading logic.

Some of its component ideas therefore have earlier cultural histories, while Human Design as an integrated system is modern. Its later development has been shaped by teaching, chart software, professional readings, and a growing practitioner community, which also means that terminology and interpretive emphasis may vary across practitioners.

Understanding this historical position helps separate the structures formally defined by Human Design from older source traditions and from later practitioner interpretation. Its continued popularity demonstrates cultural and self-observation value; it does not by itself convert every theoretical claim into an established scientific fact.

### 中文固定正文

PHI OS 把人类图视为一种关于个人运行结构与决策方式的视角，而不是对一个人的全部定义。人类图本身负责 Type、Strategy、Authority、Profile、Definition、Centers、Channels、Gates 以及已经正式接纳的进阶变量；PHI OS 不重新计算这些结果，也不会把原始图表符号直接转换成新的心理或现实事实。

在 PHI OS 中，人类图结果首先以自己的结构身份被保留。接下来，系统再区分哪些是确定的图表结果、哪些是传统或体系内解释、哪些是条件式个人读取，以及哪些内容需要客户当前生活证据才能进一步比较。

因此，一个开放中心并不自动等于某种心理问题，一条通道也不能单独证明某种职业、关系或人生结果。PHI OS 更关心的是：这套设计结构提供了什么观察角度，它是否在现实中出现，以及客户能否通过长期观察判断哪些解释真正有用。

### Fixed English Copy

PHI OS treats Human Design as one perspective on personal operating structure and decision mechanics rather than as a complete definition of the person.

Human Design remains responsible for its own Type, Strategy, Authority, Profile, Definition, Centers, Channels, Gates, and formally admitted advanced Variables. PHI OS does not recalculate these results and does not turn raw chart symbols into new psychological or real-world facts.

The Human Design result is first preserved in its own structural identity. PHI OS then distinguishes chart facts from system interpretation, conditional personal reading, and claims that require current-life evidence before they can be compared with Reality.

An open Center therefore does not automatically establish a psychological problem, and a Channel alone does not prove a career, relationship, or life outcome. PHI OS asks what observational perspective the design provides, whether that perspective appears in lived Reality, and which interpretations remain useful when tested over time.

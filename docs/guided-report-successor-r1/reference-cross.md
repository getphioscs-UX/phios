# CROSS-02｜什么是 Cross Reading？
## WHAT IS A CROSS READING?

### 页面角色
`METHOD_INTRODUCTION`

### 固定标题

**什么是交叉读取？**  
**WHAT IS A CROSS READING?**

### 固定副标题

**不是寻找“哪个方法最准”，而是把不同方法的结构放在同一张语义地图上比较。**

**Not a search for the “most accurate” method, but a structured comparison of different perspectives within a shared semantic map.**

### 中文固定正文

交叉读取不是另一种独立的命理或人格体系。它建立在已经完成的单方法读取之上，把不同方法各自已经形成、可以追溯的客户结论映射到共同的语义维度，再观察它们之间的关系。

不同方法并不观察完全相同的对象。八字强调结构与时间关系，占星通过行星、宫位与相位组织出生投影，紫微斗数通过宫位与星曜划分人生领域，数字学压缩数字模式与周期，PHI 构型描述运行结构；在正式准入后，人类图与 PHI OS Profile 也可以提供各自不同的结构证据。Cross 的目标不是把这些方法强行说成同一套理论，而是保留它们的差异，再判断哪些描述具有共同主题、哪些彼此补充、哪些存在张力、哪些只有在特定情境下才能同时成立，以及哪些问题仍然没有足够资料回答。

因此，一份 Cross Report 的价值不来自“有多少个方法说了同一句话”，而来自它能否把不同视角之间的关系解释清楚。

### Fixed English Copy

A Cross Reading is not another independent divination or personality system. It begins with already completed single-method readings and maps their traceable customer claims into shared semantic dimensions so that the relationships among those perspectives can be examined.

Different methods do not observe exactly the same thing. BaZi emphasizes structural and temporal relationships; astrology organizes a birth projection through planets, houses, and aspects; Zi Wei Dou Shu maps life domains through palaces and stars; numerology compresses numerical patterns and cycles; PHI Configuration describes runtime structure. Where formally admitted, Human Design and the PHI OS Profile can contribute additional structural evidence from their own authorities.

Cross does not force these methods into one theory. It preserves their differences and asks where they describe a common theme, where they complement one another, where tension exists, where agreement depends on context, and where important questions remain unanswered.

The value of a Cross Report therefore does not come from counting how many methods say the same thing, but from making the relationships among different perspectives intelligible.

# CROSS-03｜为什么 PHI OS 要建立 Cross？
## WHY WAS CROSS BUILT?

### 页面角色
`METHOD_ORIGIN_CONTINUITY`

### 固定标题

**为什么 PHI OS 要建立交叉读取？**  
**WHY WAS CROSS BUILT?**

### 固定副标题

**因为人往往拥有很多答案，却缺少一种方法判断这些答案之间到底是什么关系。**

**Because people often accumulate many answers without a way to understand how those answers relate to one another.**

### 中文固定正文

个人读取最常见的问题并不是没有解释，而是解释太多。一个人可能同时拥有八字、星盘、紫微、人类图、人格测评或其他结果，每一种方法都有自己的语言，而且往往会分别讨论事业、关系、资源、身份或时间。当这些结果单独存在时，客户很容易面对新的问题：哪些内容其实在描述同一个主题，哪些只是使用了相似词汇却意义不同，哪些观点彼此补充，又有哪些结论实际上互相冲突。

Cross 被建立，就是为了处理这种“多视角之后的问题”。它不是再增加一个答案，而是建立一个比较层，把各方法已经正式准入的结论保留在自己的来源之下，再映射到共同语义维度。

这使系统能够保留一致，也保留差异；能够指出多个视角共同关注了一个主题，同时避免把“多个方法说过”误写成“事实已经被证明”。

### Fixed English Copy

One of the most common problems in personal reading is not a lack of interpretation, but an excess of disconnected interpretations. A person may already have BaZi, astrology, Zi Wei, Human Design, personality assessments, or other results, each using its own language and often discussing similar areas such as work, relationships, resources, identity, or timing.

Once those readings exist side by side, a new problem appears: which statements are genuinely describing a related theme, which merely use similar language, which perspectives add something different, and which conclusions actually conflict?

Cross was built to address that second-order problem. It does not add another independent answer. It creates a comparison layer in which each admitted method claim retains its original lineage while being mapped into shared semantic dimensions.

This allows the system to preserve both agreement and difference: it can show when multiple perspectives emphasize a related theme without turning “several methods mentioned it” into “the claim has been proven.”

# CROSS-04｜PHI OS 如何看待并使用 Cross？
## HOW DOES PHI OS USE CROSS?

### 页面角色
`PHIOS_METHOD_LENS`

### 固定标题

**PHI OS 如何看待并使用交叉读取？**  
**HOW DOES PHI OS USE CROSS?**

### 固定副标题

**Cross 比较的是已经准入的方法结论，不重新计算原方法，也不把一致当成真理投票。**

**Cross compares admitted method claims; it does not recalculate the methods or treat agreement as a vote for truth.**

### 中文固定正文

PHI OS 的 Cross 层不直接读取原始星曜、干支、数字、闸门或其他方法符号，也不会重新执行各方法的计算。它只消费已经通过各自方法治理链形成的客户可发布结论，并保留这些结论的来源、条件、证据与边界。

这些结论随后被映射到共享语义维度，Cross 才判断它们之间属于共同、互补、张力、情境依赖或开放关系。多个方法共同关注同一个主题，可以提高这个主题在报告中的观察优先级，但不能因此把它升级成客观事实；相反，如果不同方法提供了不同方向，Cross 也不会为了制造漂亮的一致性而抹去差异。

PHI OS 使用 Cross 的目的，是帮助客户更清楚地看到“不同视角之间是什么关系”，再把这些关系与当前现实比较，而不是宣布哪一个方法赢了。

### Fixed English Copy

The PHI OS Cross layer does not read raw stars, stems, numbers, gates, or other method symbols directly, and it does not rerun the calculations of the underlying methods. It consumes only customer-publishable claims that have already passed through each method's own governed reading chain, preserving source lineage, conditions, evidence, and boundaries.

Those claims are then mapped into shared semantic dimensions before Cross classifies their relationship as Common, Complementary, Tension, Context-Dependent, or Open.

When several methods emphasize a related theme, that theme may receive higher observational priority in the report, but agreement does not convert the theme into objective fact. Conversely, when methods point in different directions, Cross should preserve the difference rather than forcing artificial consistency.

The purpose of Cross is to make the relationships among perspectives visible and then compare those relationships with Reality—not to declare which method wins.

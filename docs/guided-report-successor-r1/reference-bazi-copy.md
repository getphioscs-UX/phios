### 固定副标题

### 固定中文正文

八字以出生的年、月、日、时建立四柱结构，并通过天干、地支、五行及它们之间的关系，描述一个人在特定出生时间下形成的结构配置。它关注的不是单一性格标签，而是不同元素如何组合、支持、牵制与变化，以及这些关系怎样随着时间周期出现不同的强调。

在实际读取中，四柱提供基础结构，五行显示不同元素之间的分布与关系，月令与整体配置帮助判断结构所处的季节与环境背景，后续时间层则用来观察不同阶段中哪些主题被进一步放大、减弱或重新组织。

八字是一种结构化读取方法。它可以提供关于格局、关系、资源、压力与时间变化的视角，但这些结果本身并不等于一个人的全部现实，也不能单独证明某件事情一定会发生。

### Fixed English Copy

BaZi builds a four-pillar structure from the year, month, day, and hour of birth. Through the Heavenly Stems, Earthly Branches, Five Elements, and the relationships between them, it describes the configuration associated with a particular birth moment.

Rather than reducing a person to a single personality label, BaZi examines how different elements combine, support, constrain, and transform one another. The Four Pillars establish the base structure, the Five Elements show distribution and interaction, seasonal context helps frame the configuration, and timing layers show how different themes may become more or less emphasized across different periods.

BaZi is a structured reading system. It can offer a perspective on pattern, relationships, resources, pressure, and timing, but its projection is not the whole of a person's Reality and does not by itself prove that a particular event must occur.

# BAZI-03｜PHI OS 如何看待并使用八字？
## HOW DOES PHI OS USE BAZI?

### 页面角色
`PHIOS_METHOD_LENS`

### 固定标题
**PHI OS 如何看待并使用八字？**  
**HOW DOES PHI OS USE BAZI?**

### 固定副标题
**保留八字的结构与时间读取，同时把结果放回现实中检验。**  
**Preserve the structural reading, then place it back into Reality.**

### 固定中文正文

PHI OS 不把八字当作一个能够单独解释整个人生的绝对答案，而把它视为一种结构与时间视角。八字负责提供自己的计算结果与结构关系；PHI OS 不重新发明四柱、五行或时间规则，也不会因为某个命局出现某种结构，就直接把它转换成关于客户现实的确定事实。

在 PHI OS 中，八字的结果首先被保留为一个方法投影，然后再区分哪些属于确定计算结果、哪些属于解释、哪些需要结合其他证据才能成立。当客户提供当前现实、资源、关系、工作或环境信息时，系统可以进一步比较出生结构与正在发生的现实，而不是假设两者必然相同。

因此，PHI OS 使用八字的目的不是替客户决定人生，而是帮助客户看见一种结构视角：哪些主题可能值得观察，哪些关系可能需要进一步验证，以及哪些地方仍然属于未知。

### Fixed English Copy

PHI OS does not treat BaZi as a complete or absolute explanation of a person's life. It treats BaZi as one structured perspective on configuration and timing.

The BaZi method remains responsible for its own calculation, Four Pillars, Five Element relationships, and timing structure. PHI OS does not recalculate or replace those rules, and it does not automatically convert a BaZi pattern into a fact about the customer's present life.

Instead, the BaZi result is preserved as a method projection. PHI OS then distinguishes calculated structure from interpretation, and interpretation from evidence about current Reality. When present-day information is available, the system may compare the birth-based structure with the customer's actual circumstances rather than assuming that the two are identical.

The purpose is not to decide the customer's life for them, but to help them see what this perspective highlights, what deserves observation, what can be compared with Reality, and what remains unknown.

## 页面上建议固定四个状态

### `CALCULATED`
**计算结果**

来自方法确定性计算，例如四柱、五行结构、周期位置。

### `INTERPRETED`
**解释**

对结构所代表意义的读取，需要保留条件与边界。

### `CURRENT EVIDENCE`
**当前证据**

客户现在真实提供或系统已有的现实资料。

### `OPEN`
**仍待观察**

暂时没有足够证据支持的部分，不应被强行写成结论。

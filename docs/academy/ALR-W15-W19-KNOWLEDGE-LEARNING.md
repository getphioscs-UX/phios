## Canonical chain

The phase establishes five lesson-bound learning chains:

`Published Knowledge → Knowledge Projection → Teaching Explanation → Synthetic Example → Synthetic Case Study`

- **ALR-W15 Knowledge-to-Learning Projection:** five reference-only projections cover all five canonical Lessons. Every source resolves to a published node and all of that node's published article locales.
- **ALR-W16 Teaching Explanation:** five governed explanations clarify sequence, distinctions and boundaries without becoming Knowledge authority.
- **ALR-W17 Example Runtime:** five static fictional or composite examples illustrate the explanations without personal or learner data.
- **ALR-W18 Case Study Runtime:** five synthetic, non-personal analytical cases preserve observation, report, inference and unknown boundaries.
- **ALR-W19 Figure Learning Projection:** the eligibility validator is active, while the canonical registry remains empty because CAR currently has no published `FIGURE` or `DIAGRAM` asset.

An additive binding registry connects the new objects to W13 Lessons. The frozen W10～W14 registries remain byte-preserved, including their empty future-integration arrays.

## Authority boundary

- Published Knowledge is referenced, never copied, revised or replaced by ALR.
- A Teaching Explanation may sequence and clarify a published source, but cannot add an ungrounded Knowledge claim.
- Examples and Case Studies are synthetic learning content. They are not ICR canonical cases, Reality Evidence, professional client records or learner records.
- ALR may reference an eligible CAR published asset. It cannot create, approve, publish, lay out or store the asset payload.
- Candidate, fixture and unpublished assets fail closed.
- Learning completion does not create Capability State, Credential, Entitlement, assignment or Professional authority.

## Runtime posture

The registries and validators make content semantics machine-checkable. They do not activate a learner-facing Academy surface, delivery session, Practice, Assessment, scoring, progress tracking, localization runtime or persistence.

The Figure registry's zero population is a governed result, not missing work. A future projection can be admitted only after the CAR published-asset authority contains a published `FIGURE` or `DIAGRAM` and its Lesson and Objective references validate.

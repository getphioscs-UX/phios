# PVP-R1-VIS-W7 | Profile Projection IR

W7 establishes `profile-customer-visual-projection-v1` and nine figure-specific IR contracts for PFIG-001 through PFIG-009.

Truth remains owned by Profile/PPR. PVP consumes the admitted Profile/PPR visual-output authority and is projection-only. The IR must preserve source/context provenance, cannot normalize heterogeneous instruments into one universal scale, cannot invent missing values, and must render `EMPTY` or `UNKNOWN` when evidence is insufficient.

W7 is not a renderer cutover. It opens W8 renderer work only after all nine IR contracts and the executable projection builder pass machine checks.

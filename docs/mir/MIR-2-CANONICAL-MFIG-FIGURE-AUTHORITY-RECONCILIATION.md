# MIR-2｜Canonical MFIG + Figure Authority Reconciliation

MFIG-001 through MFIG-050 now have one canonical semantic identity registry. MFIG owns only method-figure identity, title, version, scope, role and identity ownership. It does not own Calculation, Projection, Interpretation, Meaning, CAR publication, renderer output, Reading, or Professional Judgment.

`content/registry/figures.json` remains the canonical Book/public figure registry and is not replaced by MFIG. CAR Figure, MFIG and MCD Dynamic Renderer remain distinct domains.

The historical MCD-6 MFIG binding v1 remains byte-preserved. A v2 successor binds renderer governance to canonical MFIG semantics without granting calculation, interpretation, meaning or production authority.

Exit requires 50 unique MFIG identities/titles, resolved authoritative versions, no unresolved authoritative figure, non-causal dependency classification, valid CAR/MCD/IR bindings, and no duplicate Figure identity authority.

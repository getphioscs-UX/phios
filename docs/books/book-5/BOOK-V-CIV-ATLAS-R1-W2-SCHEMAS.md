# BOOK-V-CIV-ATLAS-R1-W2｜Schemas & Registry Foundation

Baseline: `e8d9c5c0b222fabfae9bae0ed9abb6e098705c1f`

W2 creates the Atlas registry root and nine JSON Schemas for the manifest, evidence authority, timeline, civilization cases, comparison families, world snapshots, long-duration trajectories, transition windows, and reversal/loss atlas.

All layer registries are intentionally empty `FOUNDATION` seeds. W2 does **not** populate T00–T19, 12 cases, comparison families, world snapshots, trajectories, transition windows, scale shifts, or loss types. Those belong to W5–W11 and require their own acceptance gates.

Schemas preserve bilingual customer labels, stable IDs, explicit evidence/unknown states, non-numeric multidimensional case fields, conceptual-vs-measured trajectory authority, and poster references as references only. Unsupported aggregate civilization/loss/superiority score shortcuts are excluded.

The W2 checker compiles all schemas with AJV 2020, validates every seed registry, verifies manifest file references, and runs negative assertions against score shortcuts and incomplete trajectory authority.

## Authority split

The Atlas has four content authorities plus an explicit unknown state. **Canonical Theory** remains Part 12 under the existing Knowledge Registry. **Historical Case** belongs to the Civilization Atlas Case Registry and never becomes a Theory Node. **Evidence / Reconstruction** records documentary/series evidence and historical reconstruction with confidence and uncertainty. **Conceptual Projection** is PHI OS interpretation and must never be presented as historical fact or statistical measurement. **Unknown** remains a first-class state rather than a missing-value shortcut.

## Publication ownership

Current publication ownership is `P12 -> BOOK-5 -> /books/reality-differentiation/`. Canonical node identities are preserved even where predecessor records still show older publication-book metadata. Atlas code must consume the current seven-volume ownership authority instead of rewriting historical Knowledge Registry records.

## Layer contract

L1 remains the book/Part 12 theory reading surface. The Explorer owns only L2–L8: Timeline, Cases, Comparison, World Slices, Long Trends, Transitions, and Reversal & Loss. W1 registers these layers but keeps `customerEnabled=false`; activation occurs only in the later wave assigned to each layer.

## Evidence and unknown boundary

Customer evidence classes are `资料系列 / Evidence series`, `历史重建 / Historical reconstruction`, and `概念轨迹 / Conceptual trajectory`. Knowledge states are `UNKNOWN`, `CONTESTED`, `PARTIAL`, `RECONSTRUCTED`, and `OBSERVED`. No absence may be silently converted into zero, false, or certainty.

## Prohibited architecture

W1 creates no parallel Knowledge Master, Article Runtime, Context Resolver, Ask Runtime, Figure Resolver, Book Runtime, ranking model, collapse score, superiority score, or OCR-to-registry path. Posters remain visual orientation only.

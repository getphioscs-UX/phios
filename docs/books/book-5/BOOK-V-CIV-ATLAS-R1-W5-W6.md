# BOOK-V-CIV-ATLAS-R1-W5-W6

## Timeline + Cases

Baseline: `f7377b736910e0845dc8a8bf3bfee708f4840c9e` (`phase 11`).

W5 activates the `T00–T19` Civilization Timeline Spine on the existing Book V Atlas shell. The timeline remains an Atlas periodization rather than a claim that world history has one exclusive period scheme. Each period preserves an explicit knowledge state and can hand representative cases into the case layer.

W6 activates a twelve-case vertical slice before the future 120-case expansion. The vertical slice spans Mesopotamia, Egypt, Persia, Han, Rome, South Asia, Tang, Abbasid networks, Song, Mali, Malacca, and Industrial Britain. Case cards expose a compact customer view; the shared inspector exposes structured runtime fields and Unknown. Search, dossier selection, and the bounded compare basket are active. No ranking or aggregate civilization score is introduced.

W7+ registries remain unactivated. Comparison-family IDs and Snapshot IDs appearing in W6 are forward references only; their customer layers remain disabled until their assigned waves.

# PWS-ENTRY-W1 Public Knowledge and Free Navigation Boundary

Status: **Frozen v1.0.0**

Sequence key: `PWS-ENTRY-PUBLIC-W1`

Baseline: `getphioscs-UX/phios` `main@d92761e78a81bd9a73bb5c010c8d8813b2eab362`

## Decision

Before payment, PHI OS must remain useful without turning every path into a service funnel. Public navigation may lead to published articles, books and previews, the Thesis, the Atlas, published Figures, bounded Free Observation, Reality Journey Pass information, Professional Service information, or leaving, saving a non-formal navigation preference and returning later.

These routes are choices, not stages of a hidden paid Journey. Service information may be available, but no specific paid service becomes the default destination and a non-commercial exit always remains available.

## Free Observation boundary

Free Observation is static, preset or rule-based orientation. It does not collect a complete personal history, create a Formal Journey, write Formal Evidence, produce an Individual Reading, create professional responsibility or invoke a high-cost individual Provider. A saved public preference cannot silently become Runtime Memory or a paid Journey.

## Understanding boundary

Prediction is not understanding. Symbolic projection is not fact. General service suitability is not an individual conclusion. Public wording must remain observational, retain unknowns and preserve the Evidence Boundary.

Unpublished Figures or articles cannot be presented as published. A route becomes available only when its publication status is true; this preserves honest progressive disclosure without making service purchase the only remaining option.

## Naming compatibility

The repository already uses the display label `PWS-ENTRY-W1` for Professional authorisation and authorised data loading. That historical contract remains unchanged. This public-navigation contract uses the unique sequence key `PWS-ENTRY-PUBLIC-W1`, and its checker runs before the historical W1 checker. Neither contract replaces the other.

## Scope

This step freezes routing responsibility only. It changes no page, Runtime, Provider, persistence, payment, Entitlement or professional behaviour. Responsive, bilingual, keyboard, focus and touch acceptance remain mandatory when customer-facing routes are implemented.

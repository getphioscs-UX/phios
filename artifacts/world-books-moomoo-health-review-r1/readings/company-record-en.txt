How does a company earn revenue, sustain operations and expand?

Read two NVIDIA disclosure periods: revenue, profit, cash and assets are different measures.

Facts
Start with the company and reporting periods

The company example uses NVIDIA’s public financial disclosures. These are historical records, not live quotes or a representation of the US economy. Comparing two periods helps explain measurement and past changes, not predict a share price.

Explanation
Separate revenue, cash and continuity

Books II–IV distinguish resource flows, ongoing maintenance and the conditions of expansion. Financial statements record some outcomes; they cannot alone establish organizational resilience, future demand or guaranteed expansion.

Facts
Six measures, with their distinct bases

Amounts are USD millions. Revenue, net income, operating cash and purchases cover fiscal years; assets and equity are period-end balances. Purchases specifically include property, equipment and intangible assets. The equity ratio is shareholders’ equity divided by total assets, not ROI, ROE, ROA or ROIC.

Sources and limits
What the records can establish

Two fiscal years allow historical financial comparisons, not live pricing or share-price predictions. Moomoo PE/PB/PS remain internal; undefined ROI is not calculated. Book VII’s scale and time boundaries apply: one issuer’s periods are neither a national readout nor a personal investment outcome.

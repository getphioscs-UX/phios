Facts

Start with the company and reporting periods

The company example uses NVIDIA’s public financial disclosures. These are historical records, not live quotes or a representation of the US economy. Comparing two periods helps explain measurement and past changes, not predict a share price.

Facts

Three observations calculated from the tables

1. Revenue rose from USD60,922 million to USD130,497 million: an increase of USD69,575 million, or 114.20% (change / FY2024 revenue).

2. Compute & Networking represented 89.04% of FY2025 revenue, versus 77.81% in FY2024 (segment revenue / total revenue). This describes the disclosed mix, not future demand.

3. FY2025 net income was USD72,880 million and operating cash flow USD64,089 million, a USD8,791 million difference. Revenue records sales; net income is the accounting result after relevant costs and expenses; operating cash flow records operating cash inflows and outflows. They are not interchangeable, and the difference alone does not identify its causes.

Explanation

Separate revenue, cash and continuity

Books II–IV distinguish resource flows, ongoing maintenance and the conditions of expansion. Financial statements record some outcomes; they cannot alone establish organizational resilience, future demand or guaranteed expansion.

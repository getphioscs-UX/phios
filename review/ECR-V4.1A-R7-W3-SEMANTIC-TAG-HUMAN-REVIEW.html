Semantic Tag Canon

审核 14 个 PHI semantic dimensions。Tag 不是判断、不是 Runtime Owner、不是 customer verdict。RECOVERY 只引用 Book IV Part 8 Maintenance Runtime，不重新建立 Recovery Driver 或 ECR 独立章节。

ATTENTION

ATTENTION｜注意

PENDING

Marks what receives sustained runtime notice or observation relative to competing signals.

标记在多个讯号或条件之间，什么获得持续的运行注意或观察。

Scope

  • observational_focus
  • signal_salience
  • runtime_notice

Boundary

  • Does not mean importance, truth or urgency by itself.
  • Does not imply conscious attention when the evidence comes only from Design/background operation.

Forbidden inference

  • ATTENTION_EQUALS_IMPORTANCE
  • ATTENTION_EQUALS_TRUTH
  • ATTENTION_AUTOMATICALLY_EQUALS_PERSONALITY
SELECTION

SELECTION｜选择

PENDING

Marks that one option, signal, direction or response is being distinguished from alternatives for further runtime processing.

标记系统从多个选项、讯号、方向或回应中区分出某一项，进入下一步运行。

Scope

  • option_discrimination
  • priority_selection
  • response_selection

Boundary

  • Does not prove free choice or preference.
  • Does not itself authorize action.

Forbidden inference

  • SELECTION_EQUALS_FREE_WILL
  • SELECTION_EQUALS_PREFERENCE
  • SELECTION_TO_ACTION_WITHOUT_COMPOSITION
BOUNDARY

BOUNDARY｜边界

PENDING

Marks a distinction that separates, contains or regulates what may cross between runtime regions, roles, resources or conditions.

标记用于分隔、容纳或调节不同运行区域、角色、资源或条件之间可否跨越的界线。

Scope

  • separation
  • containment
  • permeability_control
  • role_limit

Boundary

  • Does not mean isolation.
  • A boundary may be permeable, temporary or context-dependent.

Forbidden inference

  • BOUNDARY_EQUALS_ISOLATION
  • BOUNDARY_EQUALS_PERMANENT_LIMIT
  • BOUNDARY_EQUALS_GOOD_OR_BAD
EXPRESSION

EXPRESSION｜表达

PENDING

Marks the translation of an internal or processed state into an observable, communicable or externally carried form.

标记内部或已处理状态如何转化为可观察、可交流或可由外部载体承接的形式。

Scope

  • communication
  • externalization
  • observable_form

Boundary

  • Does not guarantee accurate representation of Reality.
  • Does not equal communication skill.

Forbidden inference

  • EXPRESSION_EQUALS_TRUTH
  • EXPRESSION_EQUALS_SKILL
  • EXPRESSION_EQUALS_IDENTITY
COMMITMENT

COMMITMENT｜承诺

PENDING

Marks sustained allocation of position, time, resource or action to a selected runtime direction.

标记系统将位置、时间、资源或行动持续投入某个已选择的运行方向。

Scope

  • sustained_allocation
  • follow_through
  • resource_binding

Boundary

  • Does not imply moral obligation.
  • Does not prove permanence or success.

Forbidden inference

  • COMMITMENT_EQUALS_MORAL_DUTY
  • COMMITMENT_EQUALS_PERMANENCE
  • COMMITMENT_EQUALS_SUCCESS
ADAPTATION

ADAPTATION｜适应

PENDING

Marks a change in operating pattern that improves fit with changed conditions without requiring the system to return to a previous state.

标记系统因条件改变而调整运行方式，以提高适配度，而不要求回到原有状态。

Scope

  • operational_adjustment
  • condition_fit
  • adaptive_change

Boundary

  • Does not mean compliance or improvement in every dimension.
  • Does not prove that the adapted state is healthy or optimal.

Forbidden inference

  • ADAPTATION_EQUALS_COMPLIANCE
  • ADAPTATION_EQUALS_IMPROVEMENT
  • ADAPTATION_EQUALS_RECOVERY
REORGANIZATION

REORGANIZATION｜重组

PENDING

Marks redistribution of roles, resources, relations, boundaries or structure within an existing runtime.

标记既有运行中角色、资源、关系、边界或结构的重新配置。

Scope

  • role_redistribution
  • resource_redistribution
  • structural_reconfiguration

Boundary

  • Does not mean complete transformation.
  • Does not establish that the new arrangement is stable.

Forbidden inference

  • REORGANIZATION_EQUALS_TRANSFORMATION
  • REORGANIZATION_EQUALS_STABILITY
  • REORGANIZATION_EQUALS_IMPROVEMENT
CONTINUITY

CONTINUITY｜延续

PENDING

Marks persistence, transfer, recurrence or maintained connection of runtime across time, contexts, versions or carriers.

标记运行如何跨越时间、情境、版本或载体保持持续、转移、重复或连接。

Scope

  • persistence
  • transfer
  • recurrence
  • cross_carrier_continuation

Boundary

  • Does not prove permanence.
  • Does not imply metaphysical or biological inheritance without separate evidence.

Forbidden inference

  • CONTINUITY_EQUALS_PERMANENCE
  • CONTINUITY_EQUALS_DESTINY
  • CONTINUITY_EQUALS_INHERITANCE_WITHOUT_EVIDENCE
RECOVERY

RECOVERY｜恢复

PENDING

Marks restoration of sufficient operating capacity after loss, strain, disruption or degradation so that runtime can continue.

标记系统在损耗、压力、中断或退化之后，恢复足够运行能力以继续运作。

Scope

  • capacity_restoration
  • post_disruption_function
  • maintenance_recovery

Boundary

  • Belongs to Book IV Part 8 Maintenance Runtime authority, not a celestial Driver.
  • Does not imply return to the previous state, trauma, healing diagnosis or full restoration.

Forbidden inference

  • RECOVERY_EQUALS_DRIVER
  • RECOVERY_EQUALS_EARTH
  • RECOVERY_EQUALS_TRAUMA
  • RECOVERY_EQUALS_RETURN_TO_PAST
VALUE

VALUE｜价值

PENDING

Marks what a runtime treats as worth preserving, exchanging, protecting, prioritizing or refusing under current conditions.

标记在当前条件下，系统把什么视为值得保存、交换、保护、优先处理或拒绝。

Scope

  • worth
  • preservation_priority
  • exchange_criterion

Boundary

  • Does not establish universal morality or objective worth.
  • Does not reduce to romance, money or preference.

Forbidden inference

  • VALUE_EQUALS_MORAL_TRUTH
  • VALUE_EQUALS_MONEY
  • VALUE_EQUALS_RELATIONSHIP
RELATION

RELATION｜关系

PENDING

Marks a dependency, exchange, influence or coordinated position between two or more runtime elements.

标记两个或多个运行元素之间的依赖、交换、影响或协调位置。

Scope

  • dependency
  • exchange
  • coordination
  • mutual_position

Boundary

  • Does not automatically mean interpersonal relationship.
  • Correlation or proximity alone does not establish a meaningful relation.

Forbidden inference

  • RELATION_EQUALS_ROMANCE
  • RELATION_EQUALS_CAUSATION
  • RELATION_FROM_PROXIMITY_ONLY
INPUT

INPUT｜输入

PENDING

Marks information, resource, condition or signal entering a runtime boundary for possible processing.

标记进入运行边界、等待被处理的信息、资源、条件或讯号。

Scope

  • incoming_signal
  • incoming_resource
  • incoming_condition

Boundary

  • Input is not the same as accepted meaning, truth or action.
  • Presence of input does not prove salience.

Forbidden inference

  • INPUT_EQUALS_TRUTH
  • INPUT_EQUALS_MEANING
  • INPUT_EQUALS_PRIORITY
CAPACITY

CAPACITY｜容量

PENDING

Marks the amount or range of load, complexity, resource demand or operation a carrier or system can currently sustain.

标记载体或系统当前能够持续承担的负载、复杂度、资源需求或运行范围。

Scope

  • sustainable_load
  • resource_capacity
  • operational_range
  • carrier_support

Boundary

  • Does not equal potential, talent or worth.
  • Capacity is conditional and may change.

Forbidden inference

  • CAPACITY_EQUALS_POTENTIAL
  • CAPACITY_EQUALS_TALENT
  • CAPACITY_EQUALS_WORTH
  • CAPACITY_IS_PERMANENT
CONSTRAINT

CONSTRAINT｜约束

PENDING

Marks a condition that narrows available options, movement, resources, timing or configuration within runtime.

标记限制运行中可用选项、移动、资源、时间或配置空间的条件。

Scope

  • limitation
  • narrowing_condition
  • resource_limit
  • timing_limit

Boundary

  • Does not mean punishment or failure.
  • A constraint may enable form as well as restrict it.

Forbidden inference

  • CONSTRAINT_EQUALS_PUNISHMENT
  • CONSTRAINT_EQUALS_FAILURE
  • CONSTRAINT_IS_ALWAYS_NEGATIVE

PERSONALITY

Conscious Interface｜觉察界面

PENDING REVIEW

Canonical definition

Organizes the part of a calculated configuration that is available to conscious observation, naming, interpretation and deliberate self-reference. It describes how configuration can enter the experienced and reportable interface without claiming that awareness creates the underlying Reality.

组织计算配置中能够进入觉察、命名、解释与主动自我参照的部分。它描述配置如何进入可体验、可报告的意识界面，但不主张意识创造了底层现实。

Scope

  • self_observation
  • naming
  • reportable_experience

PENDING REVIEW

Canonical definition

Organizes the part of a calculated configuration that can continue operating through embodied, adaptive or background runtime without requiring direct conscious access. It describes a structural contribution carried by the system, not a hidden destiny or a claim that the person is unaware of it.

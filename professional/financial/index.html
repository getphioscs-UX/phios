Financial Reality Navigation 不是产品销售页，也不是泛泛的“理财建议”。它的职责是：把目标、现金流、资产、负债、保障、约束、证据与未知项组织成一个可以被复核的当前财务现实。Financial Reality Navigation is not a product-sales page or generic financial advice. Its role is to organise goals, cash flow, assets, liabilities, protection, constraints, evidence and unknowns into a reviewable current financial reality.
